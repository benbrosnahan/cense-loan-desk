# A11Y-01: Header forces sideways scrolling at 320px

**P1** · effort S · Accessibility

Below 361px wide, including a 1280px screen at 400% zoom, the header is wider than the screen, so every section with loans scrolls sideways and the hide-amounts button sits off screen.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | Low-vision borrowers who zoom to 400% and anyone on a phone narrower than 361 CSS px get a page that pans sideways on every section with loans; the hide-amounts button is almost entirely off screen and keyboard focus on it stays hidden, and on a 320px phone the stretched page can put the Undo toast after Remove, and the section bar, out of view. |
| Standard | WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.10 Reflow (AA): content must not need scrolling in two directions at 320 CSS px wide, the width of a 1280px window at 400% zoom. A header row is not covered by the exception for content that needs a two-dimensional layout. ## Measured

Shipped build, Chromium, fonts loaded:
- Your loans, Plans, Payoff and Records: document scrollWidth 361 at 320, 340, 359 and 360 wide, which is 41, 21, 2 and 1px of overflow; 0 from 361 up, including 375 and 390. First run and form errors: 0 at every width. A real pan moves the page by exactly those amounts; html, body and main are all overflow-x visible.
- Real browser zoom, no emulation: a 1280x1024 Chrome window at 400% gives innerWidth 320, devicePixelRatio 4, scrollWidth 361, and a wheel pan reaches scrollX 41. A 960px window at 300% gives the same.
- Cause: 16 padding + 150.16 brand + 12 gap + 134.88 badge + 4 gap + 44 toggle = 361.04, and neither group can shrink because whitespace-nowrap sets their minimum width. Hiding the toggle alone gives scrollWidth 320 in all four states with every disclosure and row open; nothing outside the header passes 320 (the main column ends at x 302.8 to 304).
- The hide toggle sits at x 317.03 to 361.03: 3 of its 44px on screen at 320, 23 at 340, 43 at 360.
- Keyboard: Tab from the page top reaches the toggle as the second stop, and Chrome does not scroll it into view (scrollX still 0 after 1.5 s at 320, at 340 and at real 400% zoom), so only an arc of the focus ring shows. Enter still works. WebKit's focus() does scroll it into view (scrollX 58).
- Phone emulation at 320x640: the layout viewport stretches to 362x723. The fixed Undo toast after Remove sits at y 651 to 703 on a 640px screen, 0% visible when the page is at the top or the last swipe was upward. After scrolling down, the sticky section bar is 0% visible at 320, 46% at 340 and 97% at 360.
- Desktop WebKit (WKWebView): identical geometry and scrollWidth 361. Live deployment: scrollWidth 361 at 320 with the same class strings.
- Under SC 1.4.12 text-spacing overrides the seeded states reach 420 at 320 and 375, and first run 372 at 320, with nothing clipped or overlapped.

## Required

At 320 CSS px wide, no sideways scrolling: document.documentElement.scrollWidth equals clientWidth on every section, and every control, including the hide toggle, is on screen without panning (WCAG 1.4.10). The fix should leave widths that pass today alone, including the one-row 64px header at 375.

## Reproduce

1. In desktop Chrome, open http://localhost:3100 (or the live site) and click 'Look around with example loans'.
2. Make the page 320 CSS px wide: zoom a 1280px-wide window to 400% (Ctrl or Cmd and +), or use the DevTools device toolbar at 320 wide.
3. On Your loans, Plans, Payoff and Records the page scrolls sideways by 41px, and the eye button at the top right ('Hide dollar amounts') shows only a 3px sliver. Press Tab twice from the top of the page: focus lands on that button, but the page does not scroll to it.
4. Compare 360 wide (1px of overflow) and 361 (none). The empty first-run screen never overflows.
5. Phone effect, in DevTools device mode at 320x640: open a loan, swipe back up a little and press Remove. The Undo toast is drawn below the bottom of the screen.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "A11Y-01"`.

## Fix

Let the header wrap only below the width that overflows, and take 2px off the gap so 360 still fits on one row. Two class strings in src/app/page.tsx; lines 324, 335 and 341 stay as they are.

```tsx
// page.tsx:323
<header className="mx-auto flex max-w-3xl items-center justify-between gap-2.5 px-4 pt-5 max-[359px]:flex-wrap sm:px-6 sm:pt-7">

// page.tsx:334 (ml-auto keeps the badge and toggle on the right when they wrap to a second row)
<div className="ml-auto flex flex-shrink-0 items-center gap-1">
```

Measured on a real build of exactly these strings (Tailwind 3.4 generates max-[359px]: and ml-auto from page.tsx): overflow 0 at 13 widths from 320 to 1280, and in 30 of 30 state and width combinations (320, 340, 359, 360, 375) with every disclosure and row open; at 320 the toggle sits at x 260 to 304 and is fully visible when tabbed to; in phone emulation at 320x568, 320x640, 340x640, 359x640, 360x640 and 375x667 the Undo toast and the section bar stay on screen; WebKit overflow 0; the print header is unchanged. Cost: below 360 the seeded header grows from 64 to 102px and the section bar moves from y 349 to 387; first run wraps only at 320 to 327 (51.2 to 89.2px); from 360 up the header stays on one row as today, the toggle moves at most 2px left up to 375, and nothing changes from 377 up.

## Severity

A measured WCAG 2.2 AA failure (1.4.10 Reflow) on all four sections of the core flow, on every visit by low-vision borrowers who zoom a 1280px screen to 400% and by anyone whose phone is narrower than 361 CSS px. It is a low P1: no dollar figure or main-column text is cut off, but keyboard focus on the toggle stays off screen and, on a 320px phone, the stretched page can hide the Undo toast and the section bar.

Proposed P1; independent severity judges said P1; final P1.

## Verification

Confirmed by 5 of 5 skeptics across 2 filings (standards, reproduce and impact on the accessibility filing; standards and reproduce on the resilience filing), in Chromium at real 400% zoom and in phone emulation, in desktop WebKit, and on the live deployment.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

Severity: the accessibility and resilience reproduce skeptics rated it P2 (only the optional hide toggle needs the pan, every figure is on screen at scrollX 0, and the overflow tapers to 1px at 360). The accessibility standards and impact skeptics kept P1, and the resilience standards skeptic raised that filing from P2 to P1, citing the AA failure on every working section, the focus ring left off screen, and the Undo toast and section bar off screen on small phones; the impact skeptic called it a weak P1 and the first to demote if P1s are ranked by reach, which this app cannot measure. SC 1.4.12: the accessibility filing listed it as a second failed criterion; the standards and impact skeptics ruled the text-spacing widening a side effect, since nothing is clipped or overlapped. Wording: 'loses the toggle' and 'pan sideways on every section' overstate it (the toggle is reachable by panning and works from the keyboard, and at desktop zoom only the toggle needs the pan), while both filings missed the hidden focus ring and the phone toast and section bar. Cause: the resilience reproduce skeptic showed flex-shrink-0 is not the binding constraint (overriding it leaves 41px); whitespace-nowrap sets the groups' minimum widths. Fix: the three skeptics who tested the accessibility filing's plain flex-wrap found it also wraps the header at 361 to 377, which pass today (375-wide phones go from 64 to 104px, and on 375x667 the second loan row drops below the fold), and found that dropping flex-shrink-0 at line 324 changes nothing. Both resilience skeptics rejected that filing's fix (hide the words 'On your device' below 375) because it removes the product's top-of-page privacy statement at widths that do not overflow, including first run at 320 to 374, and at 360 to save 1px; the footer note at page.tsx:841 repeats it, but far below the first screen. The skeptics proposed four other working variants; see notes.

Fix choice: the accessibility impact skeptic's variant, built and measured. It is the smallest change that clears 320 to 360 without touching widths that pass today, and it keeps the badge's words. Other variants that cleared the overflow: (1) the accessibility reproduce skeptic's (flex-wrap gap-x-1.5 gap-y-1 sm:gap-3 on the header, ml-auto on the right group, badge px-2.5 sm:px-3, -mr-2 sm:mr-0 on the toggle), built, which also keeps first run on one row at 320 but moves the toggle 8px toward the screen edge at every phone width; (2) the resilience reproduce skeptic's flex-wrap gap-x-2 gap-y-1 min-[360px]:flex-nowrap sm:gap-x-3, measured, which leaves the wrapped badge and toggle left-aligned; (3) the resilience standards skeptic's !empty && "max-[360px]:flex-wrap" with ml-auto, built, which also wraps at 360 (92px) to remove 1px; (4) the accessibility standards skeptic's flex-wrap gap-x-2 gap-y-1 with ml-auto, tested in the live DOM rather than a Tailwind build, which wraps every seeded header below 374 (96px). Trade-offs of the chosen fix, measured: the text-spacing widening at 360 to 412 remains (418 against 420 shipped), which is not a 1.4.12 failure; a 3px overflow remains in a 360px window with a forced 4px classic scrollbar (about a 1440px window at 400% zoom), outside the 320 requirement. The README's layout sweep covered 375, 768, 1024 and desktop, so 320 was never checked. Not tested: iOS Safari, Firefox and real phones; phone results are Chrome device emulation and the WebKit result is desktop WKWebView. Related: A11Y-13 (the toast covering focus) and RES-15 (a long nickname pushing Undo off screen).
