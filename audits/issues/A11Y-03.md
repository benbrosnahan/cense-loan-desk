# A11Y-03: Focus is lost to the page after common actions

**P2** · effort M · Accessibility

After 19 of 37 common actions, including every Next card and saving or cancelling a loan edit, keyboard focus falls to the page instead of a nearby control, so keyboard and screen-reader users lose their place and phone users are left with the edited loan off screen.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | Keyboard and screen-reader borrowers lose their place after most actions that change the view: after each Next card Chrome sends the next Tab back up to the section bar, after saving or cancelling an edit it goes to 'Add a loan' rather than the loan just changed, and after Undo it leaves the page; screen readers are given no focused element at all (what they then read was not measured). Phone users who save an edit to a lower loan are left with that row, and its new balance, above the screen. Mouse and touch users are not affected by the focus loss itself. |

## Measured

Shipped build; every control reached with real Tab presses and activated with real Enter or Space, a fresh browser context per action:
- document.activeElement is BODY after 19 of 37 action types; 11 keep focus and 7 move it somewhere meaningful. Identical on Chrome 127, 146 and 150, at 1280x900 and 375x812; all 19 also end on BODY in WebKit (WKWebView). The accessibility tree reports only the page root (RootWebArea) as focused after each. Each of the 19 really completed (tab, row count, toast and downloaded files checked).
- Where the next Tab lands in Chromium: after the Next cards and Choose a plan, on 'Your loans' in the section bar; after Remove, on the following row; after Save or Cancel in the form, on 'Add a loan', not the edited row; after Use 5.5%, on the servicer select; after first-run Cancel, on 'studentaid.gov', past the button that opened the form; after the first loan is saved, on 'Your loans'; after example loans, on 'Add my own'; after the top pick, on 'This is my plan' in the plan list; after Clear every loan, on 'Add my first loan'; after Clear saved records and Records Download, on the footer link 'Cense'; after Loans Download, on 'Print one page'; after Undo, out of the document.
- Presses needed to reach the natural target (1 means no extra cost), Chromium at 1440x900: Next cards and Choose a plan 5; Edit Save or Cancel 4 (second row); first-run Cancel 5; Undo 11; the other 11 actions 1. WebKit: Next cards and Choose a plan 1, Remove 2, Edit 4, first-run Cancel 5, Undo 8, and three restart at 'Skip to content': Clear saved records 21, Download on Loans 18, Download on Records 9.
- The Chromium landing on 'Your loans' after Next cards comes from goTo's scrollIntoView on the sticky bar (page.tsx:211): with that call stubbed out, or aimed at the wrapper under the bar, the next Tab lands on the new section's first control.
- Editing on a phone, 375x812, last of 8 loans: Edit jumps the page -579px to the form on a 4,044px page. After Save: scrollY 1482, edited row top -425, focus on BODY, toast 'Changes saved'. Tapping Save as soon as it scrolls into view instead leaves the row at -99 to -25, balance still hidden. Across all 8 rows the new balance is visible after Save for 2 of 8 at 812px tall and 1 of 8 at 664px. Desktop 1440x900: the row stays on screen (top 367), focus still on BODY.
- Live site (GETs only): the Next cards and Records Download behave the same.

## Required

When an action removes or disables the focused control, focus moves to the control that opened the content or to a logical neighbour (F85), never to the document, so the next Tab continues from the result of the action. After saving or cancelling an edit, the edited row is in view and focused, so the new balance can be checked. Mouse and touch users see no change.

## Reproduce

1. Open the app, choose 'Look around with example loans', and from here use only the keyboard.
2. Tab to the 'See which repayment plans you can use' card at the bottom of Your loans and press Enter. Plans opens, but document.activeElement (DevTools console) is <body>, and in Chrome the next Tab goes back up to 'Your loans' in the section bar (5 presses to the first Plans control in the probe data).
3. Back on Your loans, open the second loan row, Tab to Edit, press Enter, change the balance and press Enter on 'Save changes'. Focus is on <body>, and the next Tab lands on 'Add a loan' above the list, not on the loan you edited.
4. The same happens with Cancel, Remove, 'Use X%' in the form (change the rate so the form offers it), Clear every loan, and Undo on the toast (after Undo the next Tab leaves the page).
5. In a phone-sized view (DevTools, 375x812), open the last loan, tap Edit, change the balance and tap 'Save changes': the edited row is above the top of the screen.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "A11Y-03"`.

## Fix

Add one page-level focus request that each action sets and React applies after it re-renders, aimed at small targets that do not capture clicks. This is the accessibility impact skeptic's tested patch, with loan rows targeted by a new id.

```tsx
// src/app/page.tsx, below sectionRef (:135), above the !hydrated return
const pendingFocus = useRef<string | null>(null);
useEffect(() => {
  const want = pendingFocus.current;
  if (!want) return;
  pendingFocus.current = null;
  // The target can be gone, for example Undo pressed after changing section.
  (document.querySelector<HTMLElement>(want) ?? document.querySelector<HTMLElement>("#section"))?.focus();
});
const rowSel = (id: string) => `#row-${id}`;

// goTo (:205). Only the Next cards (:618, :794, :822) and onGoToPlans (:816) pass true;
// the section bar's own buttons keep their focus.
const goTo = (next: Tab, moveFocus = false) => {
  if (moveFocus) pendingFocus.current = "#section";
  setTab(next);
  // ...rest unchanged
};

// :546, first child of the keyed div; the div itself stays unfocusable
<h2 id="section" tabIndex={-1} className="sr-only">{TABS.find((t) => t.id === tab)!.long}</h2>
```

- Loans form Cancel (:574): pendingFocus.current = editing ? rowSel(editing.id) : "#add-loan"; first-run Cancel (:391): "#add-first".

## Severity

Every keyboard and screen-reader borrower runs into this on most visits, but nothing is blocked: in Chromium 11 of the 19 actions already send the next Tab to the natural control and the rest need 3 to 10 extra presses, and the WCAG 2.4.3 failure is clear only where the inline loan form closes. Phone users who save an edit to a lower loan usually cannot see the new balance without scrolling to find it; raise to P1 if a screen-reader check shows the reading position resetting to the top.

## Verification

Confirmed by 5 of 5 skeptics across 2 filings (reproduce, standards and impact on the accessibility filing; reproduce and standards on the UX filing), with real key presses in Chromium 127, 146 and 150 and in WebKit, and on the live site for the actions checked.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

Severity: the accessibility reproduce skeptic kept P1, because the 8 actions with a real detour include every move through the core sequence. The accessibility standards and impact skeptics lowered it to P2: nothing is blocked, the cost where the form closes is 0 to 8 extra presses, the worst losses are WebKit-only or off the core flow, and screen-reader harm is unmeasured. Both UX skeptics kept that filing at P2. Standard: the standards skeptic found SC 2.4.3 failed only where the loan form closes (Edit Save, Edit Cancel and first-run Cancel clearly; Add Save, Add Cancel and first loan saved literally but with little harm), with 9 of the 19 not failures at all and the 'activeElement is never body' rule coming from advisory APG guidance; the UX standards skeptic treated 2.4.3 as advisory for an inline form. Impact wording: 'restart from the top' and 'lose their place after most actions' did not reproduce for keyboard users in Chromium, where the next Tab continues near the old position, and after Next cards WebKit already lands in the new section. Edit target: the standards skeptic would return focus to the row's Edit button, the literal F85 trigger; the other tested patches use the row button, which this report keeps because its accessible name includes the updated balance. Remove: the standards skeptic noted Chromium already lands on the next row, so that target is polish. Filed fix, rejected by all three accessibility skeptics: it does not compile (long is not in scope at page.tsx:546, TS2304); with that repaired, Use X% and Clear every loan still end on body, as do removing the only loan and Undo after either Clear or after changing section; focusing the whole section scrolls the example-loans view from y 0 to 431 (447 at 375) and pushes the 'These are example loans' banner off screen; tabIndex -1 on the whole section captures clicks, so click-then-Tab jumps the page from 957 to 94; a page-level request for Use X% goes stale and later pulls focus back to the rate field; and swapping disabled for aria-disabled without styles drops the disabled look. The UX filing's fix, placed at the end of saveLoan, broke 'Save and add another' (form top -1438) and used loan, which is not in scope in Cancel.

Fix choice: the accessibility impact skeptic's patch. Measured on that build: 0 of 19 actions lost in Chromium and WebKit; the edge cases above all get a target; the example view stays at y 0; click-then-Tab matches shipped; mouse paths show no focus ring; a double Enter on Download still makes one export; axe reports 0 violations on all five views, as shipped. It was chosen over the reproduce and standards skeptics' patches, which also reached 0 lost in their runs, because those make the whole section div focusable, which the impact skeptic measured capturing clicks and moving the Tab starting point. One change from the tested patch: rows are targeted by a new id on the row button (row-<loan id>) instead of [aria-controls="loan-<id>"], because A11Y-16 proposes removing aria-controls from closed rows, which would silently break the Remove target. It is the same element, but this selector was not built. Focusing the row without preventScroll also covers the UX filing: the edited row comes back on screen on phones. The UX skeptics' alternative (scrollIntoView({ block: "center", behavior: scrollBehavior() }) then focus({ preventScroll: true }), only in saveLoan's else branch and the edit Cancel) also tested well if a smooth scroll is wanted (row at 409 to 483 on a phone, focused). Coordination: A11Y-02 adds a say() to the same three Undo restores, so keep both lines. A11Y-04 proposes focusing Undo when its toast appears; the page's effect runs after the Toast's, so with both shipped the Remove and Clear targets here win and Undo never gets focus (measured in a combined build). Decide the two together: either drop the Remove and Clear targets here and set targets on Undo or Dismiss, or keep Undo out of the focus path. UX-03 (moving goTo's scroll target off the sticky bar) removes Chromium's 4 extra presses after Next cards on its own. A11Y-05 announces section changes from goTo, and with this fix a Next card also focuses the section heading, so check the two together for a double announcement. A11Y-17 (heading outline) should account for the new visually hidden h2. Not tested: any real screen reader (including whether these focus moves cut off a polite toast), Firefox, Safari itself (the WebKit runs used WKWebView, and Safari skips buttons on Tab unless full keyboard access is on) and iOS.
