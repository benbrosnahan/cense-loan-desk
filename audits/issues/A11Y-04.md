# A11Y-04: Undo expires at 7 seconds and never pauses

**P2** · effort S · Accessibility

The Undo after removing a loan or clearing data disappears after a fixed 7 seconds, even while focused or hovered, so a keyboard or switch user who cannot Tab the 6 to 13 stops to it in time must retype the loan, and cleared records are lost for good.

## Measured

Timer: page.tsx:172 gives any toast with an action 7,000 ms and nothing pauses it. From Remove to toast gone took 7,000 to 7,016 ms in every run (20 by one skeptic, 18 by another, at 375 and 1440): untouched, with keyboard focus on Undo, with the pointer parked or moving on it, and after both Clear actions. The filer's single samples were 7,013, 7,016 and 7,026 ms. A plain toast lasts about 2.6 s (2,603 ms). If focus is on Undo when it expires, focus drops to BODY.
Distance: with eight loans, Undo is 13 Tab presses away after removing the first row, 10 after the fourth and 6 after the last (rows below plus 6), 5 after Clear every loan and 2 after Clear saved records, the same at every width tested (375, 412, 1280, 1440). It grows with the list: 17 Tabs at 12 loans, 30 at 25.
Pace (real key presses at a steady rate; noisy shared machine): from the first row Undo was reached at 350 to 480 ms per press and missed at 450 to 600 ms; from the last row reached at 600 to 950 ms and missed at 800 to 1,050 ms; Clear every loan reached at 800 to 1,100 ms and missed at 1,000 to 1,399 ms; Clear saved records reached at up to 2,200 ms and missed at 2,500 to 2,800 ms. At one press a second Undo is out of reach from every loan row, and Clear every loan is borderline.
No other way back: Remove, Clear every loan and Clear saved records change storage within 40 to 60 ms with no confirmation. After expiry 7 of 8 loans stay stored, also after a reload, and no section has a restore, import or file input. Retyping the removed loan takes 26 key presses and gives it a new id (LoanForm.tsx:205), so Records lists it as both removed and added (diffSnapshots matches on id, storage.ts:75); a cleared dated record cannot be recreated, because makeSnapshot stamps the current time (storage.ts:59).
Touch at 375: Undo is in view and one tap restored all 8.

## Required

SC 2.2.1: a time limit set by the content can be turned off, adjusted or extended (with a warning and at least 20 seconds to extend), unless it is real-time, essential or longer than 20 hours; a timed message is exempt only when the user has another means of performing the same function. SC 3.3.4: deleting user data is reversible, checked or confirmed. Here that means an Undo that stays until the borrower uses or dismisses it, or a confirmation before the data goes.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 with no saved data and click 'Look around with example loans' (8 loans).
2. Using only the keyboard, Tab to the first loan row (Freshman subsidized), press Enter to open it, Tab to Remove and press Enter. A toast at the bottom says 'Removed Freshman subsidized' with an Undo button.
3. Press nothing for 8 seconds. The toast and its Undo are gone, the heading reads 'Your 7 loans', and a reload keeps 7.
4. Repeat, now pressing Tab about once a second toward Undo (13 presses from the first row in the probes' eight-loan set). The toast disappears before you get there. Parking keyboard focus or the mouse pointer on Undo does not keep it either.
5. Open Keep a copy > Clear my data and press Clear every loan. There is no confirmation, every loan goes at once, and the Undo lasts the same 7 seconds.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "A11Y-04"`.

## Fix

Give an Undo no deadline, let the borrower close it, and make the two Clear buttons ask first instead of offering an Undo that would keep cleared data in memory for the next person. No new dependency.

1. src/app/page.tsx:169-174, timer on plain toasts only:
// An Undo has no timer: it stays until used, dismissed or replaced.
useEffect(() => {
  if (!toast || toast.action) return;
  const t = setTimeout(() => setToast(null), 2600);
  return () => clearTimeout(t);
}, [toast]);

2. First line of goTo (page.tsx:205), so an open Undo ends when the borrower changes section and plain toasts keep their 2.6 s:
setToast((t) => (t?.action ? null : t));

3. Toast, src/components/shared/ui.tsx:589-600: wrap the action button in a fragment and add a Dismiss button after it (an icon keeps the pill 64 px tall at 320):
<button type="button" aria-label="Dismiss" onClick={onDismiss} className="flex h-10 w-10 flex-shrink-0 items-center justify-center rounded-full text-lg leading-none text-white transition-colors hover:bg-white/10 focus:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-white"><span aria-hidden>×</span></button>

4. src/app/page.tsx:261-272, clearing confirms and keeps nothing:
const clearLoans = () => {
  if (!window.confirm(`Clear all ${loans.length} loans from this browser? This cannot be undone.`)) return;
  setLoans([]);
  setTab("loans");
  say("All loans cleared");
};
const clearSnapshots = () => {
  if (!window.confirm(`Delete all ${snapshots.length} saved records from this browser? This cannot be undone.`)) return;
  setSnapshots([]);
  say("Records cleared");
};
Update 'undo on remove and on clear' at README.md:202.

Do not move focus into the toast (see notes). Ship together with A11Y-13, because a pill that stays makes its overlap with focused controls last.

## Severity

It is a real WCAG 2.2.1 Level A failure, but it only affects keyboard, switch and screen-reader borrowers who remove or clear something by mistake and then need more than about 0.5 s per key press (first of eight rows) to about 1 s (last row) to reach Undo. That is an uncommon recovery path outside the loans, plan, payoff and records sequence, pointer and touch users reach Undo in one tap, and a lost loan can be retyped, so the harm is bounded (P2, not P1).

## Verification

Confirmed by 5 of 5 skeptics across 2 filings (reproduce, standards and impact on the accessibility filing; reproduce and standards on the resilience filing); 4 of 5 rated it P2.
