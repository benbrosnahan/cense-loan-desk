# A11Y-09: Loan names and rates truncated

**Not filed.** Its only member, a11y-truncated-loan-name-rate, split 1 confirmed to 1 refuted, and the tie-breaker did not confirm it. The clipping is real: at 320, 4 of the 8 example names are cut, and with WCAG text spacing 12 spans are cut, including a rate shown as '4…'. But the refuting standards skeptic found no failure of SC 1.4.10 or 1.4.12, because Understanding for both accepts truncation when another view reveals the content, and here it does: Payoff's 'Your order' list shows every full name and rate unclipped at 320 and 375, even with text spacing (src/components/ranking/RankingPanel.tsx:468 and :471), Edit shows them in two or three taps (a long name scrolls inside its field), and the row's accessible name holds the full text. Both skeptics also found errors in the filing: 'row height stays 74px' came from a probe that measured only the first row (cut rows wrap to 98px at 320, and to 120px with text spacing), and 'clean at 768 and above' holds only for loans paid out before July 1, 2026. Left for the author as optional polish, not a filed issue: swapping truncate for break-words at src/components/loans/LoanList.tsx:53 and :56 was built and cleared every cut span, at the cost of taller rows (the 8-loan list grows from 601 to 697px at 320). The same truncation cuts the ' · after Jul 1, 2026' flag in a post-cutoff loan's row on phones 320 to 393 wide, but the expanded row (src/components/loans/LoanList.tsx:102) and the warning at the top of Loans (src/app/page.tsx:550) both state it.
