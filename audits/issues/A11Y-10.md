# A11Y-10: Two text colours below 4.5:1

**P2** · effort S · Accessibility

The saved-records count in the section bar and the placeholder in a loan field that failed validation are below the 4.5:1 contrast minimum, so low-vision borrowers may not be able to read them.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | Low-vision borrowers may not be able to read how many snapshots they have saved, or the '0' and '0.00' format hints in a field that has just failed validation. The README's claim that all text meets AA (README.md:74) is currently not true. |
| Standard | WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), Level AA |
| Where | Section bar, the count after 'Records': on the Loans, Plans and Payoff tabs once at least one snapshot is saved, at rest and on hover, at 375 and 1440 wide (it passes while Records itself is open). Add a loan form after a failed save (form-errors state): the placeholders in 'What you owe on it today' (#balance) and 'Interest rate' (#rate), at 375 and 1440. |
| Code | [`src/app/page.tsx:534`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L534), [`src/components/shared/ui.tsx:291`](../../src/components/shared/ui.tsx#L291), [`src/components/shared/ui.tsx:294`](../../src/components/shared/ui.tsx#L294), [`README.md:74`](../../README.md#L74) |
| Test | `a11y-details.spec.mjs`: "A11Y-10 the saved-records count in the section bar and the hints in empty loan fields read at 4.5:1, at rest, on hover and after a failed save" |

## Measured

Count '2' (opacity .75; 14px/600 at 375, 16px/600 at 1440) on Loans, Plans and Payoff at rest: slate-800 composited to #638966 on #FFFFFF, 3.96:1 (3.98:1 unrounded, 4.00:1 from real pixels #638866). Settled hover: #58815C on #EAF3E7, 3.92:1 computed and 3.93:1 from pixels, at both widths. With Records open it passes: #C9D6CB on #275B2D, 5.33:1. Placeholders '0' and '0.00' (16px/400) after a failed save: #5C7F5F on the invalid fill #FFF9F9, 4.33:1 from pixels at 375 and 1440 (4.34:1 unrounded); the same placeholder in a valid field is 4.51:1 on white. axe 4.11.2 reports 0 violations: it marks the one-digit count incomplete ('Element content is too short to determine if it is actual text content') and does not check placeholders; with 12 snapshots, '12' becomes an axe violation at 3.96:1. A composite walker over 13 states at both widths found 60 non-exempt text pairs: 57 pass and these 3 fail.

## Required

4.5:1 for text smaller than 18.66px bold or 24px regular. The count is 14 to 16px semibold and the placeholder 16px regular, so 4.5:1 applies, and the threshold is not rounded, so 4.33:1 fails. Neither item falls under an exception (large text, inactive control, incidental text, logotype).

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 with empty storage and click 'Look around with example loans'.
2. Click Records in the section bar and press 'Save today's snapshot' twice. While Records is open the count passes (5.33:1).
3. Click 'Your loans' ('Loans' on a phone). The '2' after Records is slate-800 at 75% opacity: #638966 on white, 3.96:1. Hover the Records tab: #58815C on #EAF3E7, 3.92:1. Plans and Payoff show the same.
4. In a fresh private window (no saved loans), click 'Add my first loan', then 'Save loan' without typing anything. 'What you owe on it today' and 'Interest rate' turn invalid, and their placeholders '0' and '0.00' sit on the faint pink invalid fill: #5C7F5F on #FFF9F9, 4.33:1.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "A11Y-10"`.

## Fix

Two class changes, no new tokens.

1. src/app/page.tsx:534: drop the opacity so the count uses the tab's own text colour.
```tsx
<span className="tnum ml-1">
  {snapshots.length}
</span>
```
Measured in a throwaway build: 7.30:1 at rest (slate-800 on white), 7.05:1 on settled hover (slate-900 on slate-100), 8.01:1 while Records is open (white on #275B2D).

2. src/components/shared/ui.tsx:291: one step darker for placeholders.
```ts
"placeholder:text-slate-600 transition-colors",
```
5.79:1 on white and 5.56:1 on the invalid fill. Leave the invalid fill at ui.tsx:294 as it is.

## Severity

Affects low-vision borrowers in two peripheral spots: the count shows only after a snapshot is saved and only on the Loans, Plans and Payoff tabs, and the failing placeholder only in an empty field that has just failed validation, where the passing label and error message already say what to enter. It is a real AA miss that also makes README.md:74 untrue, but no text a borrower needs to finish a task is hard to read, so it stays P2 rather than the P1 kept for an AA failure in a core flow.

## Verification

Confirmed by 2 of 2 skeptics (standards, reproduce); both kept P2.
