# A11Y-11: Landmarks nested in main; skip link inside main

**Not filed.** Not confirmed. The only member, a11y-landmarks-skip-link, split 1 confirmed (reproduce) to 1 refuted (standards), and the tie-breaker did not confirm it. The facts reproduce: the header, footer and skip link sit inside <main> (src/app/page.tsx:310), so there is no banner or contentinfo landmark, and the skip link points at a div with no tabindex (page.tsx:354). No WCAG criterion fails, though. axe 4.11.2 passes bypass, region, landmark-one-main, landmark-main-is-top-level and skip-link. SC 1.3.1 treats landmarks as a sufficient technique, not a requirement. SC 2.4.1 is met by the skip link, the landmarks and the headings. Following a link to a non-focusable target correctly moves the sequential focus starting point, so the next Tab lands in the content in Chromium and WebKit. The keyboard harm was also overstated: from the focused skip link, Enter then Tab and Tab then Tab both reach the first section button, so the link saves no presses rather than one. The filed fix regressed when built: tabIndex=-1 on <main> made clicking plain text and then pressing Tab jump the page back up (scrollY 835 to 0 in Chromium), and moving the footer out of <main> let the sticky section bar scroll off screen at the page bottom on short viewports such as 320x568. What survives is optional P3 landmark hardening with no WCAG claim.
