# A11Y-13: Toast covers focus and swallows taps

**P3** · effort S · Accessibility

The bottom toast can completely cover the control a keyboard user has just tabbed to on the way to Undo, and a plain 'Saved' toast swallows taps on whatever sits under it for 2.6 seconds.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | A keyboard borrower tabbing to Undo after removing a loan can lose sight of where focus is for a stop or two on a tall phone or at high zoom, and a borrower who taps a field or button under a 'Saved' toast within 2.6 seconds loses the tap (nothing typed and nothing saved wrong). |
| Standard | WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), AA. A sticky notification that entirely hides the focused control fails, but Note 2 excuses content the user opened when focus can be revealed without moving it (one or two ArrowUp presses, or the 7-second expiry, do that here), so today's toast arguably passes; a toast that stays up would not. The tap interception breaks no standard. |
| Where | Your loans with the audit's 8-loan portfolio (user-owned ids, so no example banner), after Remove. The toast with Undo lasts 7 seconds and Undo is the last Tab stop on the page (the Toast renders last in main, page.tsx:862), so a keyboard user passes the rest of the page to reach it. Fully covered stops at 390x928, 390x944 and 390x960 (sixth row), 430x932 (fourth and sixth rows) and 360x800 (eighth row), and at the zoom-sized viewports 320x256 (every row) and 342x192; partial elsewhere, worst 0.80 at 1440x900. Taps: any width, for 2.6 seconds after Save loan, Save and add another, a plan choice or a snapshot; at 375x812 the pill is y 748-792, x 82-293. Code: no bottom scroll padding (globals.css:5-8); the pill takes pointer events even with no button (ui.tsx:583 and :586); timers of 2.6 s and 7 s (page.tsx:170-174). |

## Measured

Focus, production build, real Tab presses after Remove:
- 84 natural paths (widths 1280 and 390, heights 640 to 960, rows 3 and 6), started both from a script-focused row and from a pure keyboard start at the top of the page: the same 4 stops are fully covered in 3 paths. The 'Cense' footer link at 390x928 (link y 872-890 under pill y 856-908), 390x944 and 390x960; 'Download as a spreadsheet' at 390x960 (y 896-936 under 888-940).
- Worst partial overlap on the six filed paths: 0.80 at 1440x900 ('Print one page'), 0.73 at 320x568, 0.63 at 375x667.
- Not in the filing: 'Cense' at 430x932 (rows 4 and 6) and 'Download as a spreadsheet' at 360x800 row 8 (0.996) among 136 device-viewport paths; at 320x256 'Print one page' is covered after removing any of the 8 rows, and 342x192 adds 16 more covered stops (24 covered stops in 16 of 64 zoom and landscape paths).
- The Undo toast lasted 7.03 s. Focused against blurred pixel diff: 0 changed pixels at 390x928, 390x944 and 430x932, so nothing of the focus shows; a 3px ring strip shows above the toast for 'Cense' at 390x960. One or two ArrowUp presses reveal the control without moving focus.
Taps, 375x812, real touch:
- The pill has computed pointer-events auto with no button, box y 748-792 and x 82-293 for the longest message (3.05% of the screen), lasts about 2.6 s (2,601 to 2,614 ms), has no handlers, and a tap does not dismiss it.
- A tap that lands on it goes nowhere: focus stays on BODY and typing 52000 leaves the income field empty. With income blank Plans shows Standard $136.44; with 52,000 and household 2 it shows RAP $166.67.
- On the natural route (save, then tap Plans) a centred tap on the income field lands and stores 52,000. The filed point (197,788) needs the finder's CDP scroll nudge of 13px.
- Share of the settled pill that sits over a control (standards skeptic): after Save and add another, 29% at 375x812 (balance label), 82% at 390x664 (Subsidized and Unsubsidized), 86% at 1440x900 (servicer select); after save then Plans, 71% at 375x812 and 84% at 320x568 (income field), 82% at 390x664 (employer buttons); 0% after Save loan and after a plan choice. The reproduce skeptic found 'Save loan' and 'Add more detail' under the pill after Save and add another. Undo stays tappable (8 loans to 7 to 8).
Fixes as measured:
- The :has() rule in the fix, production build: 0 fully covered stops on the 6 filed viewports, the short and zoom viewports (100 paths; worst partial 0.354 at 342x192 and 0.213 at 320x256), 136 device-viewport paths and 168 grid paths. With no toast it does not match, and focus walks at 320x256, 375x667 and 1280x720 match the shipped build.
- The pointer-events change, scratch build: elementFromPoint at the pill's centre is outside [role=status], the tap focuses income and keeps 52,000, and Undo still restores the loan.

## Required

A focused control is never entirely hidden by the toast (SC 2.4.11), including at 320x256, the 400% zoom reflow reference; a toast without a button lets taps through to what is beneath it, and a toast with Undo stays tappable.

## Reproduce

Focus:
1. Load the audit's 8-loan portfolio on http://localhost:3100. By hand: 'Add my first loan', then 'Save and add another', using the school-year shortcuts (they fill the date and rate) and these balances: 2022–23 subsidized 3,500 and unsubsidized 2,396; 2023–24 4,500 and 2,326; 2024–25 5,500 and 2,256; 2025–26 5,500 and 2,123. Then on Plans enter income 52,000 and choose Standard.
2. Set the window to 390x960 (DevTools device toolbar) and open Your loans.
3. Using only the keyboard, Tab to the sixth row ('2024–25 unsubsidized'), press Enter to open it, Tab to Remove and press Enter. 'Removed 2024–25 unsubsidized' with Undo appears at the bottom.
4. Keep pressing Tab toward Undo, the last stop on the page. 'Download as a spreadsheet' and later the 'Cense' footer link take focus while completely under the toast, with no focus ring in sight; one or two ArrowUp presses reveal them. At 390x928 and 390x944 the 'Cense' link is hidden the same way, and at 320x256 removing any row hides 'Print one page'. Which stop is hidden depends on the page height, so other data can move it to a neighbouring viewport.
Taps:
5. At 390x664 with no saved data, click 'Add my first loan', tap '2024–25', enter 12000 and click 'Save and add another'. Within 2.6 seconds tap the part of the Subsidized or Unsubsidized button that the dark 'Saved ...' pill covers: the tap lands on the pill and nothing is selected. At 375x812 the pill covers part of the balance label, at 1440x900 the servicer select.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "A11Y-13"`.

## Fix

Two small changes, no dependency.

1. src/app/globals.css, after the html rule at :5-8. While a toast is showing, scroll focused controls clear of it; the rest of the time nothing changes, because the Toast renders nothing when empty (ui.tsx:578).
```css
/* While a toast is up, keep a focused control clear of it (WCAG 2.4.11). */
html:has([role="status"] > div) {
  scroll-padding-bottom: 6rem;
}
```

2. src/components/shared/ui.tsx:585-587. Only a toast with a button takes taps. The outer wrapper is already pointer-events-none (ui.tsx:583), so a plain 'Saved' pill lets taps through and the Undo pill stays tappable.
```tsx
<div
  className={cn(
    "rise flex items-center gap-1 rounded-full bg-slate-900 py-1.5 pl-5 pr-1.5 text-sm font-semibold text-white shadow-lift",
    toast.action && "pointer-events-auto"
  )}
>
```

## Severity

Keyboard borrowers who remove a loan near the end of the list on a tall phone screen or at 400% zoom pass one or two controls hidden under the toast on the way to Undo, and touch borrowers who tap a control under a plain 'Saved' toast within 2.6 seconds lose that one tap. Nothing is lost or wrong, an arrow key or the 7-second expiry reveals the control, and SC 2.4.11 arguably passes for a toast the user triggered, so it is polish today; it stops being transient if A11Y-04 keeps Undo up.

## Verification

Confirmed by 3 of 3 skeptics across 2 filings (a P3 review on the focus filing; standards and reproduce on the tap filing), with real Tab presses and touch taps on the production build; both tap skeptics lowered P2 to P3.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

Focus filing: the P3 reviewer reproduced every number, also from a pure keyboard start, but rejected the filed fix, @media (min-height: 400px) { html { scroll-padding-bottom: 6rem; } }. The guard switches the padding off at 320x256, the WCAG reflow reference (1280x1024 at 400%), where the toast fully covers 'Print one page' on all 8 rows and 24 stops in the zoom suite; full cover is what SC 2.4.11 forbids, while the partial overlap under the sticky bar that the guard was meant to avoid passes. The reviewer also noted that Note 2 may excuse today's user-triggered toast, which supports P3 without refuting the measurement. Tap filing: both skeptics lowered P2 to P3. The filed point (197,788) and 'Standard $136 instead of RAP $167' come from the finder's agent route, whose CDP scrollIntoViewIfNeeded on the stuck section bar nudged the page 13px. On the natural route a centred tap on the income field lands and stores 52,000; the reproduce skeptic got 0 interceptions in 16 attempts there (it measured a different resting position, with the field at y 473), and stock Playwright and Puppeteer click, tap and fill were not blocked. The 'first mobile run' is not on disk: ax-agent-mobile.json records 52,000 after 4 attempts that hit the pill text. A person loses one tap, not typed text, because no phone keyboard opens without focus, and a blank income is disclosed (PlansPanel.tsx:129-147 asks for income instead of showing IBR or RAP). The natural collision is Save and add another, so the test should cover that path.

Fix choice: the reviewer's :has() rule over the filed media-query guard, for the reason in dissent; it was run on a production build across every suite listed in measured. The pointer-events change is the filed one, built and checked on a scratch copy by the standards skeptic. Ship with A11Y-04: its fix keeps the Undo toast up until it is used or dismissed, which turns this overlap from 7 seconds into a lasting one, and its Dismiss button makes the pill taller at narrow widths; the 6rem padding was measured only against today's pill, so re-run the 320x256 walk if both land. A11Y-02 keeps the role=status container mounted and renders the pill only while a toast exists, so the [role="status"] > div selector still matches only while a toast shows (not built together).
