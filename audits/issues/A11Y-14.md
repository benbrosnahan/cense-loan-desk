# A11Y-14: Field names, units and required state

**P2** · effort S · Accessibility

The add-a-loan form hides its % and $ units and which fields are required from screen readers and agents, and saves a rate typed as 0.0653 without a word, so a $12,000 loan shows a $100 Standard payment instead of $136.

## Measured

Chromium accessibility tree, production build, 375 and 1280 wide:
- Units: the rate field is named 'Interest rate' in every state and its % suffix is aria-hidden. Its description has no unit when blank or for Grad PLUS ('Federal rates are fixed, so this never changes.') or after a school year fills it ('We filled in the published undergraduate rate for 2024–25. Change it if yours is different.'); only an undergraduate loan dated from July 1, 2022 with a different rate typed gets 'The published undergraduate rate for 2024–25 was 6.53%. Use 6.53%'. The $ prefix is aria-hidden the same way, so 'What you owe on it today', 'Yearly income, before tax' (value '52,000') and 'Extra each month' carry no unit.
- Fraction rate: 0.0653 saves with no alert, for Subsidized and for Grad PLUS. Stored rate 0.0653, row '0.07%', and on a $12,000 balance Standard shows $100 a month and $12,040 in total against $136 and $16,373 at 6.53% (engine $100.33 against $136.44). Lowest rate in the app's own table: 4.99%.
- Run-together names: 'Nameoptional', 'Amount you first borrowedoptional', 'Minimum monthly paymentoptional', and 'Records2' on the section bar (fixed in A11Y-15). No other control matched in the add form or the Loans, Plans and Payoff tabs.
- Required: #balance and #rate report required false with aria-required absent; #disb (role Date) and #servicer report no required property. Nothing visible marks a required field; with 'Add more detail' closed no field says optional, and 'Who do you pay?' has no optional mark although its hint says it is fine to leave for later. Leaving an empty field announces its error on blur (role=alert, aria-invalid true). name is set on 0 of the 7 form fields.
- Slider: role slider, name 'Extra each month, slider', value 100, empty valuetext, max 500. After typing 2000 the slider and its accessibility value stay at 500 (also at 505, 1,000 and 5,000). One ArrowLeft then sets the extra to 495 ('$495 extra a month gets you out 6 years 10 months sooner'), a drop of $1,505 from one key press; a click mid-track sets 250.
Fixes as measured:
- A space before 'optional' and a screen-reader-only unit in the label, production build: 'Name optional', 'What you owe on it today in dollars', 'Interest rate percent', 'Yearly income, before tax in dollars', 'Amount you first borrowed optional in dollars', 'Extra each month in dollars', 'Who do you pay? optional'; the run-together scan comes back empty.
- Rate floor, scratch build: 0.0653 is refused with 'Enter the rate as a percent, like 6.53, between 1 and 25.' and nothing is stored, for Subsidized and Grad PLUS; 6.53 saves; prefilled rates are unaffected.
- Required: aria-required gives #balance and #rate required true; native required does the same, a blank Save still shows the app's 3 alerts and focuses #disb with nothing stored, and the date input shows no required state in Chrome's tree under either attribute. With names, aria-required and aria-valuetext added on a scratch build, verify.mjs passed 180 of 180 and axe reported no new violation.
- Slider with the corrected range, production build: valuetext '$100 a month'; after typing 2000, ArrowLeft gives 1,995 and ArrowRight 2,000 again; a drag to the 90% point settles at 1,795 to 1,800 and stays; the $200 preset returns the slider to 200 of 500.

## Required

Each field's unit is part of its accessible name in every state; names have word breaks; the three required fields expose required state, and Servicer is marked optional like the other skippable fields; the slider is named without its role, gives its value in dollars, shows a typed amount above $500 and keeps it when nudged; and a rate below 1%, lower than any real rate the skeptics found (2.75% for 2021–22 Direct Loans), is flagged with a message that says to enter a percent, without blocking the save (see RES-14).

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 with no saved data and click 'Add my first loan'.
2. Tap '2024–25', type 12000 in 'What you owe on it today', replace the rate with 0.0653 and click 'Save loan'. It saves with no message and the row reads '0.07%'. On Plans, Standard shows $100 a month and $12,040 in total; at 6.53% it is $136 and $16,373.
3. Open the add form again, pick Grad PLUS and type 0.0653 as the rate: the hint reads only 'Federal rates are fixed, so this never changes.', and the loan saves the same way.
4. With a screen reader, or in Chrome DevTools > Accessibility, focus each field: the rate is announced 'Interest rate' with no 'percent', the balance has no 'dollars', none of the date, balance and rate fields is announced as required, and after opening 'Add more detail' the names read 'Nameoptional', 'Amount you first borrowedoptional' and 'Minimum monthly paymentoptional'.
5. Choose a plan, open Payoff and type 2000 in 'Extra each month'. The slider sits at its end and is announced as 500, named 'Extra each month, slider'. Focus it and press ArrowLeft once: the extra becomes $495.
Probes.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "A11Y-14"`.

## Fix

Four small edits, no dependency, nothing sent anywhere.

1. src/components/shared/ui.tsx, Field (:236-287). A space before 'optional', and a unit only screen readers hear, because the $ and % beside the input are aria-hidden (:314, :332).
```tsx
// Field's props
/** Spoken after the label, because the $ or % beside the input is hidden from screen readers. */
unit?: "dollars" | "percent";

// :256-264
<label htmlFor={htmlFor} className="block text-sm font-bold text-slate-900">
  {label}{" "}
  {optional && (
    <span className="ml-1.5 font-normal text-slate-600">optional</span>
  )}
  {unit && (
    <span className="sr-only">{unit === "percent" ? " percent" : " in dollars"}</span>
  )}
</label>
```
Pass unit="dollars" to the Fields for the balance (LoanForm.tsx:328), first-borrowed amount (:456), minimum payment (:476) and income (page.tsx:723), and unit="percent" to the rate (LoanForm.tsx:350). The extra-payment label is a plain label, so at RankingPanel.tsx:234 write: Extra each month<span className="sr-only"> in dollars</span>

2. src/components/loans/LoanForm.tsx. Mark the three required fields natively; the form is noValidate (:227), so no browser bubble appears and the app's own messages still run. Mark Servicer optional like the other skippable fields. A rate typed as a fraction, such as 0.0653, is left to RES-14's hint, which asks the borrower to check any rate under 1% without blocking the save.
```tsx
<input id="disb" type="date" required ... />                     // :295
<AffixInput id="balance" prefix="$" required ... />               // :334
<AffixInput id="rate" suffix="%" required ... />                  // :380
<Field label="Who do you pay?" htmlFor="servicer" optional ...>   // :398
```

3. src/components/ranking/RankingPanel.tsx. Name the slider without its role, give its value in dollars, and let it show amounts above $500 without dropping them when nudged. The range changes only on typed or preset input, never while the slider moves.
```tsx
// after :98
const [sliderMax, setSliderMax] = useState(() => Math.max(SLIDER_MAX, extra));
// :148
const sliderValue = Math.min(extra, sliderMax);
// :244-248, the typed field
onChange={(e) => {
  const n = Math.min(EXTRA_MAX, parseNumber(e.target.value));
  setSliderMax(Math.max(SLIDER_MAX, n));
  onExtraChange(n);
}}
// :253-267, the slider
aria-label="Extra each month"
aria-valuetext={`${money(extra)} a month`}
max={sliderMax}
style={{ "--fill": `${(sliderValue / sliderMax) * 100}%` } as React.CSSProperties}
// :280, the presets
onClick={() => { setSliderMax(SLIDER_MAX); onExtraChange(p); }}
```

4. The run-together 'Records2' on the section bar is fixed by A11Y-15's change to the same button.

## Severity

It reaches every screen-reader borrower and form-filling agent who adds a loan or sets an extra payment: units, word breaks and required state are missing every time, and the slider reads 500 for any extra above $500. The SC 1.3.1 gap sits in the core add-a-loan flow, which on its own points to P1, but it blocks no step, and the one wrong figure (a Standard payment $36 a month too low) needs an unusual entry: a rate typed as a fraction, which saves silently but then reads 0.07% in the loan row. Both rate skeptics kept P2, one because the wrong figure needs an unusual entry and the other because few people are hurt, and A11Y-07, an AA failure in the same form that blocks no step, is P2 as well.

## Verification

Confirmed by 6 of 6 skeptics across 5 filings (reproduce and standards on the rate filing, a P3 review on each of the other four), in Chromium at phone and desktop widths; none changed a severity, and 5 of the 6 corrected the filed fix.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

Rate filing: both skeptics found 'no unit in its accessible name or description' too broad: for an undergraduate loan dated from July 1, 2022, typing a different rate brings the description 'The published undergraduate rate for 2024–25 was 6.53%. Use 6.53%'. That is a passive cue: no error fires, and Grad PLUS or older dates get none, so the $36 understatement stands. Both rejected the filed hint sentence, which reached only the last of three hint branches; the reproduce skeptic wanted the unit in the label ('Interest rate (%)', not built), the standards skeptic an unconditional hint sentence (built, unit present in every state). experiments.json C.rateAsFraction reads accepted:false, a probe artifact: it counted Next's permanent empty route-announcer alert, while the same record's savedRow and the stored data show the rate saved. Names filing: the filed unit prop on Field cannot reach the extra-payment field, whose label is a plain label (RankingPanel.tsx:230); corrected. Required and field-metadata filings: both skeptics refuted 'only after a failed save', because leaving an empty field announces its error on blur (LoanForm.tsx:303-305, :346 and :393, with ui.tsx:269). Chrome's tree shows no required property for the date input or the servicer select under either attribute, so the filed accessibility-tree test cannot pass for #disb. The field-metadata reviewer would not call required state a demonstrated SC 3.3.2 failure, because the form marks optional fields; found 'no stable key' overstated (11 of 12 fields have an id and all 12 an accessible name); and preferred native required to aria-required. Slider filing: the filed max = Math.max(SLIDER_MAX, extra) regressed. With the maximum following the value, ArrowRight cannot climb back (1,995 stays 1,995) and a mouse drag decays from 1,980 to 655 in 13 moves, which is worse than today for anyone above $500; corrected to a range that changes only on typed or preset input.

Fix choices. (1) Units go in the label as screen-reader-only text through a unit prop on Field, the names filing's fix corrected to reach the extra-payment field, not in the hint: an error replaces the hint (ui.tsx:266-284), the filed hint sentence reached one of three hint branches, and the label route covers the five dollar fields too. A visible 'Interest rate (%)' would also work but was not built. (2) Native required rather than aria-required: both measured the same in Chrome's tree, native needs no ARIA, the form's noValidate keeps browser bubbles away, a blank Save still runs the app's messages, and it also fills the required list of a declarative WebMCP schema (3 fields against none). (3) The slider uses the reviewer's corrected range, because the filed one ran away. Typing 1,003 leaves the slider at 1,000 (step 5) while its valuetext says $1,003. (4) name attributes on the 7 form fields are left out: they only feed the declarative WebMCP form route, which AGENT-01 recommends leaving out, and every field already has an id and an accessible name. autocomplete=off on these fields is right, since no autofill token fits a loan. (5) The 1% floor: both skeptics kept it. The lowest real rates they found were 2.75% (2021–22 Direct Loans) and 1.72% (2021 FFEL, from a search summary only), and the form offers only Direct loan types. Overlaps to settle as one change: MONEY-22 and UX-16 rewrite the same message at LoanForm.tsx:180 ('above 0 and up to 25'), and RES-14 adds a non-blocking 'looks unusual' hint for rates under 1. If this floor ships, use its message and drop RES-14's rate hint; its balance hints stand. MONEY-22's alternative of accepting 0% would need its own exception. If RES-05's backup restore lands, its parseBackup must apply the same floor; its draft check (rate > 0) accepted 0.0499. Cosmetic: the space before 'optional' widens the visible gap by one space; drop ml-1.5 if that matters (not measured). Each part was built and measured separately, not as one patch. Measured in Chromium's accessibility tree only; no real screen reader was run, which is why the filing says screen readers 'may' run the words together. Changed on 2026-11-04, while writing the tests: the fix no longer refuses rates under 1%. That refusal would fail RES-14's test, whose check must never block saving, and the guard in tests/money-edges.spec.mjs that saves 0.01% exactly as typed. RES-14's non-blocking hint covers the fraction rate instead, and the A11Y-14 test passes with a refusal, a warning or a corrected value.
