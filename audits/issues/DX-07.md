## Measured

A second dollar formatter at calc.ts:480-486 beside utils.ts:10-17; the utils.ts one prints '—' for null, undefined and non-finite values, the calc.ts copy has no guard. A date-time formatter at export.ts:16-24 beside utils.ts:71-83. Balance and principal sums written inline at storage.ts:61, ranking.ts:92 and export.ts:63, although totalBalance and totalPrincipal already exist at calc.ts:28-34. Near-identical month formatters at utils.ts:86-100. One ineligible-plan return block copied three times at calc.ts:284-300, 309-320 and 324-335, differing only in the reason and in years (0, 0 and 25). The plan count typed as 6 at page.tsx:616 and PlansPanel.tsx:393; README.md:118-120 records the earlier hard-coded '5 of 6' for the count that page.tsx:199 now computes and line 616 shows. The skeptic applied the fix in a copy: tsc 0, lint clean, verify.mjs PASS 180, build ok, text identical over 12 runs, 0 page errors; the only style change was the <p> at PlansPanel.tsx:393, 0.016px wider because JSX splits the text node.

## Required

One definition for each rule that can change: one dollar formatter, one balance total and one principal total, a plan count read from the plan list, and one shape for a closed plan.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 and click 'Look around with example loans'.
2. On Loans, the 'See which repayment plans you can use' card reads 'N of the 6 federal plans are open to you'.
3. Open Plans: beside the plan list heading it reads 'N of 6 federal plans'. N comes from the engine, but both 6s are typed into the JSX (page.tsx:616, PlansPanel.tsx:393), so adding or removing a plan would leave them stale. The other copies give the same output today and show nothing in the app; read the listed lines.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "DX-07"`.

## Fix

Derive the count, reuse the existing totals and formatter, and fold the three closed-plan returns into one helper. Leave the date formatters for now (see notes).
```tsx
// src/app/page.tsx:616 and src/components/plans/PlansPanel.tsx:393; add PLAN_ORDER to each file's existing @/data/plans import
body={`${eligiblePlanCount} of the ${PLAN_ORDER.length} federal plans are open to you.`}
{open.length} of {PLAN_ORDER.length} federal plans
```
