# MONEY-06: Payoff ignores the chosen plan's schedule and forgiveness

**P0** · effort M · Money figures and eligibility

Payoff runs every plan as one flat payment for up to 50 years, so Graduated borrowers are told their 10-year plan never ends, some IBR and RAP borrowers get debt-free dates years or decades past forgiveness, and extra payments are shown saving years they do not save.

## Required

One plan, one answer on every tab. Payoff runs the selected plan's own schedule: Graduated steps up every two years and is repaid within 120 months; IBR stops at payment 240 and RAP at 360, with RAP's interest waiver and $50 matching principal, and what is left is shown as forgiven. At $0 extra, Payoff's end month, total paid, interest and forgiven amount equal the Plans card. When the required-only path ends in forgiveness, extra payments are compared on total paid, not on months or interest.

## Fix

Pass the selected plan's schedule into the Payoff engine as an optional last argument, so the flat call in verify.mjs (compareStrategies(port, 125, 100)) still works, and print the Plans card's own figures at $0 extra so the two tabs cannot disagree.
src/types/index.ts:
export interface PlanSchedule {
  paymentAt: (month: number) => number; // Graduated's steps, flat otherwise
  termMonths: number;      // forgivenessYears * 12 on IBR and RAP, years * 12 on fixed plans
  forgivesAtTerm: boolean;
  rapRules: boolean;       // (h)(4)(i) waiver and (o)(2)(i) match
  simpleInterest: boolean; // IBR, the same rule as MONEY-05
}
src/lib/calc.ts: split an exported graduatedSchedule(balance, rate) out of graduatedTotals (calc.ts:89) with paymentAt = (m) => base * Math.pow(g, Math.min(4, Math.floor(m / 24))), and add export function planSchedule(e: PlanEstimate, ctx: PlanContext): PlanSchedule.
src/lib/ranking.ts, simulate(loans, strategy, requiredPayment, extra, schedule?):
const maxMonths = schedule?.termMonths ?? 600;                // replaces :82
let due = schedule ? schedule.paymentAt(m) : requiredPayment; // each month; replaces :84 and :118
const charged = schedule?.rapRules ? Math.min(accruedTotal, due + extra) : accruedTotal;
if (schedule && !schedule.forgivesAtTerm && m === maxMonths - 1) due = Math.max(due, owed - extra); // fixed plans finish in term
const principalDown = Math.max(0, paidNow - charged);
if (schedule?.rapRules && principalDown < 50) reduceProRata(Math.min(Math.min(50, paidNow) - principalDown, open)); // payment made, never negative

## Severity

A borrower who follows the app's own Graduated recommendation (the 'Lowest monthly payment' pick for the example loans at every income from $42,000 up) to Payoff, the next step in the main sequence, is told the balance never clears whenever the first month's interest is above $25, and IBR and RAP borrowers whose income sits near their debt (on $40,000 at 6.53%, IBR incomes of $51,200 to $59,800) get dates past forgiveness. On the slider a borrower headed for forgiveness can be told $25 a month saves 12 years 10 months and $27,430 when it saves no time and costs $6,000 more; the only caveat on screen says 'you would finish sooner than shown' under a headline that shows no date, so no level is dropped.

Proposed P0; independent severity judges said P1, P0; final P0.

## Verification

Confirmed by 9 of 9 skeptics across 3 filings (reproduce, standards, impact on each); all kept P0.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

No skeptic lowered the severity; the Graduated filing's standards skeptic weighed P1 (the error overstates the debt and the flat payment is disclosed in small print) and kept P0. Corrections adopted: 'every income-driven borrower' is wrong, since most IBR and RAP grid cells are right or right in substance and the harm is a band plus the slider; 'fixed plans agree apart from rounding' is wrong on mixed-rate portfolios (Extended up to 63 months past term), which belongs to the separate multi-loan finding; the Graduated scope is wider than filed (75 of 91 grid cases contradict the card, and Graduated is also the lowest-payment pick at $52,000 and $100,000 income, not only with income blank). All three filed fixes fail as written: the IBR/RAP fix's Math.min(50, required) match goes negative with extra, and its interest tile would show $16,616 against the card's $12,387; the Graduated fix finishes the example loans in 121 months (failing its own test) and leaves 'stays at $139 the whole way'; and the UX fix's bare maxMonths = term * 12 makes Standard print 'does not clear' over a $27.59 remainder. The skeptics proposed four different replacements (see notes). The regulation does not settle whether RAP extra counts toward the $50 test (685.211(a)(2) calls extra a prepayment); the fix models the conservative reading, under which $18,000 at $100 + $25 clears in 254 months rather than 212.

Fix choice: the plan-schedule variant from the UX filing's standards skeptic. It is the smallest tested option because the Plans card and its picks do not change. It was built on a scratch copy with tsc clean, next build passing and verify.mjs 180/180; 9,680 plan, income and portfolio cases had 0 past term and 0 fixed plans uncleared, and the page printed Standard November 2036 $9,199, Graduated November 2036 $12,545, IBR at $45,000 'Forgiven in November 2046' $11,216 and RAP at $45,000 'Forgiven in November 2056' $2,825. Two corrections from the other filings are folded in: IBR unpaid interest kept in its own per-loan map (MONEY-05's rule), and total-paid wording when the required-only path ends in forgiveness. Alternatives: the one-engine patch also closes the multi-loan gap but reprices the card per loan and moves the top pick in 41 of about 20,000 portfolios, so take it only together with the multi-loan finding; a pooled projectPlan for the headline was tested only at engine level and stops the headline responding to the avalanche/snowball toggle; per-loan Graduated levels are needed only if the author wants month 120 to the dollar on mixed-rate portfolios. Dependencies: land MONEY-05 in the same change (with only one side changed, IBR forgiven amounts differed on 633 of 1,584 points, worst $489,561), and MONEY-04 with it so the stepped schedule is the compliant one (4.53x today).
