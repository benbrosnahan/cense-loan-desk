# MONEY-23: Plan count omits PAYE and ICR

**P1** · effort S · Money figures and eligibility

The plan count ('4 of the 6 federal plans') leaves out PAYE and ICR, which ED still offers until July 2028, so borrowers with older loans are told fewer plans are open than ED offers and some PSLF borrowers are told a costlier plan is their lowest PSLF payment.

## Measured

Example loans (the shared 8-loan portfolio: $28,101, paid out 2022-25) at $52,000, single: the Loans card says '4 of the 6 federal plans are open to you.'; Plans says 'All 4 plans open to you' and '4 of 6 federal plans' (Standard, Graduated, IBR, RAP). ED's list for the same loans adds ICR and PAYE (PAYE $233.83, below the $310.83 ten-year amount): 6 of 8. Over 4 older-loan portfolios x 144 profiles (576 rows), the app is short by 2 in 448 and by 1 in 128, and matches ED in 0. PAYE prices exactly like the app's IBR card (0 of 384 rows differ), so leaving PAYE out moves no figure or pick. ICR does move the pick: in 24 of 192 forgiveness-branch profiles it is $11.03 to $50.62 a month below the app's pick, $221 to $1,012 over the 20 payments left before ICR ends. Example: same loans, $65,000, single, nonprofit: the hero says 'Standard $311' and '...this is the lowest payment among the plans that actually earn Public Service Loan Forgiveness credit', while ICR is $271.21 ($39.62 lower) and counts for PSLF. A wider grid (5,640 PSLF-branch profiles) found ICR below the pick in 597 (10.6%), by $0.06 to $56.36 a month. Two skeptics rebuilt the ICR model independently and reproduced ED's 50 sample payments in 91 FR 34815 within $1. The live bundle (page-eae479cdacf15b74.js) contains the same strings.

## Required

The count and the 'lowest payment among the plans that actually earn' claim must either cover every plan the borrower can choose today or say they cover only the plans compared here and name the missing ones. ED's pages list 8 plans and offer ICR on Direct Loans paid out before July 1, 2026, and PAYE to new borrowers whose payment is below the 10-year amount, until both end no later than July 1, 2028; 91 FR 34815 says borrowers may choose ICR 'until such plan sunsets on July 1, 2028'. ICR payments count for PSLF through June 30, 2028 (685.219(b)(28)(iv)). Even under the codified 685.209(c)(4)(iv) and (c)(5)(i)(B), which still limit new PAYE and ICR enrollment to borrowers on them on July 1, 2024, 'the 6 federal plans' is wrong: current enrollees keep those plans through June 30, 2028 ((c)(7)(i)(F)), ED lists 8 plans, and 685.209(a) plus 685.208 name at least 9.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 (or https://cense-loan-desk.vercel.app/) with no saved data and click 'Look around with example loans' (8 loans, $28,101, paid out 2022-25).
2. On Loans, the 'See which repayment plans you can use' card says '4 of the 6 federal plans are open to you.'
3. Open Plans: the heading says 'All 4 plans open to you', the line beside it says '4 of 6 federal plans', and PAYE and ICR appear nowhere.
4. Answer 'Going for forgiveness' and 'Nonprofit', type 65,000 as yearly income and leave household at 1: 'Where we would start' shows Standard $311 with '...the lowest payment among the plans that actually earn Public Service Loan Forgiveness credit'. ICR for these loans is $271.21.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "MONEY-23"`.

## Severity

Every borrower whose loans all predate July 1, 2026 and include their own loans sees a count that leaves out two plans ED still offers, though for most of them no figure or pick changes. The decision-changing harm, a false 'lowest PSLF payment' claim worth $11.03 to $50.62 a month for up to 20 payments, falls on a recognizable group: forgiveness or public-service borrowers for whom ICR is cheaper (24 of 192 profiles in the finder's grid, 10.6% in a wider one).

Proposed P1; independent severity judges said P1; final P1.
