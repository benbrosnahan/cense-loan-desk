# UX-01: Choosing a plan reads as enrolling

**P2** · effort S · UX and UI

Picking a plan says 'Standard is now your plan' though it only changes the numbers in this app, and nothing tells the borrower they still have to apply at studentaid.gov or ask their servicer, so they can leave thinking their plan has changed.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | A borrower can leave believing the app has put them on a plan and never apply; a bill usually corrects that, but one in grace or forbearance gets no bill to correct it, and one on the $0 IBR path who stops paying is delinquent the next day. |
| Standard | Nielsen H2, match between system and the real world: 'your plan' means the servicer's plan in the app's own copy (plans.ts:170, :182) and in federal usage, yet here it labels a setting stored only in this browser. H1, visibility of system status, in part: feedback is prompt but states more than the app did (types/index.ts:118 calls the field the plan 'chosen or is modelling'). No WCAG criterion fails. |
| Where | Plans tab at every width checked (320, 375, 768, 1024, 1440), on the local build and the live site: the toast, the navy 'Where we would start' box, the chosen plan card, the summary under 'You owe' (on all four tabs and in print) and the Payoff header. Worst on the card path, when the chosen plan is not our pick: no pill renders and no disclaimer is on screen. |

## Measured

Example or fixture loans (8 loans, $28,101). After choosing in the hero: toast 'Standard is now your plan' (role=status, about 2.6 s on screen), pill '✓ This is your plan', card badge '✓ Your plan', card button '✓ This is my plan', summary '... · $311 a month on Standard' on all four tabs, Payoff header 'ON STANDARD · $311 A MONTH REQUIRED'. With every disclosure open, the Plans tab has 0 matches for apply, enroll, sign up, switch or 'does not change'. In src, 'apply' appears only as 'apply anything above the scheduled amount' (RankingPanel.tsx:500), 'different rules apply' (plans.ts:255) and a MIME type (export.ts:82). Other hints exist, but none says the real plan is unchanged or names a step: 'Your servicer sets your actual payment' 20px under the pill (hero path only), the footer's 'studentaid.gov is the authority on your account', 'On your device' in the header, and 'This changes your selected plan everywhere in Loan Desk' behind a closed disclosure (RankingPanel.tsx:375). The page's only studentaid.gov link (PlansPanel.tsx:483) is labelled 'Loan Simulator' and called a model; that URL now resolves to the federal Repayment Calculator, which holds the Apply step. Card path at 375 (IBR chosen while Standard is our pick): toast 'IBR is now your plan', no pill, the hero note 1,110px above the viewport, nothing mitigating on screen. Income $20,000 with 'Lowest monthly payment': toast 'IBR is now your plan' and '$0 a month on IBR' atop every tab. Post-cutoff portfolio: 'RAP is now your plan' on a page that says Tiered Standard is 'what you get automatically if you never pick a plan' (plans.ts:170). Choosing sends 0 network requests and only writes profile.selectedPlan to localStorage; the live page chunk holds the same strings. A widened check fails 12 of 12 paths and widths on the shipped build.

## Required

Wherever a plan is chosen, the page says the choice only changes the numbers shown here and names the real step: apply at studentaid.gov or ask the servicer. No toast, label, summary or header states that a plan is the borrower's actual plan.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 (or https://cense-loan-desk.vercel.app/) in a fresh browser at phone width and click 'Look around with example loans'.
2. Open Plans and leave the four questions as they are. In the navy 'Where we would start' box, click 'Use Standard as my plan'. Read the toast ('Standard is now your plan'), the pill ('✓ This is your plan'), the Standard card ('✓ Your plan', '✓ This is my plan') and, scrolled up, the line under 'You owe' ('... · $311 a month on Standard'). Search the page for 'apply' or 'enroll': nothing.
3. Open Payoff: the header reads 'ON STANDARD · $311 A MONTH REQUIRED'.
4. Back on Plans, click 'Use Graduated as my plan' on the Graduated card: toast 'Graduated is now your plan', no pill, and the box's disclaimer is scrolled off screen.
5. Choose 'Lowest monthly payment', type 20000 as yearly income and click 'Use IBR as my plan': toast 'IBR is now your plan', and every tab's summary reads '$0 a month on IBR'.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-01"`.

## Fix

Say what the choice does and name the real step wherever a choice is made. Copy plus one paragraph, in 3 files.

src/components/plans/PlansPanel.tsx:
```tsx
// top of file: the federal tool whose Apply button puts a borrower on any plan
const APPLY_URL = "https://studentaid.gov/repayment-calculator/";

// :124
{selected && <Badge tone="brand">✓ In my numbers</Badge>}
// :208
{selected ? "✓ Using this for my numbers" : `Use ${meta.name} for my numbers`}
// after :225, inside PlanRow: a card choice has no pill, and the hero is off screen
{selected && (
  <p className="mt-3 text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-700">
    This only changes the numbers here. To actually get on a plan, apply at{" "}
    <a href={APPLY_URL} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-semibold text-navy underline underline-offset-2 hover:text-navy-hover">studentaid.gov</a>{" "}
    or ask your servicer.
  </p>
)}
// :365, the pill: ✓ In my numbers
// :373: Use {PLAN_META[top.estimate.id].name} for my numbers
// :377-380, the hero note, which renders before and after a choice:
An estimate, not financial advice. Choosing a plan here only changes these
numbers. To actually get on a plan, apply at{" "}
<a href={APPLY_URL} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="font-semibold text-white underline underline-offset-2">studentaid.gov</a>{" "}
or ask your servicer, who sets your actual payment.
```
src/app/page.tsx:785: say(`Showing your numbers on ${PLAN_META[id].name}`); and :470: if you are on {PLAN_META[selectedEstimate.id].name}
src/components/ranking/RankingPanel.tsx:171: Using {PLAN_META[selected!.id].name} ·{" "}  and :375: This changes the plan used for your numbers everywhere in Loan Desk. It does not change your plan with your servicer.

Do not copy the first-run link classes (page.tsx:405) into the hero: text-navy on bg-navy measures 1.00:1. The white link measures 8.01:1 on the hero and the navy link 8.01:1 on the white card. No dependency; the links only open studentaid.gov in a new tab.

## Severity

Every borrower who picks a plan sees the status wording ('is now your plan', '✓ Your plan', and '$311 a month on Standard' atop every tab), but each figure is a correct estimate and no misreading was measured, so this is a misleading statement with no wrong number. No level is dropped: the hero's 'Your servicer sets your actual payment' never says the real plan is unchanged and is off screen on the card path.

## Verification

Confirmed by 3 of 3 skeptics (reproduce, standards, impact); reproduce and standards corrected severity to P2, impact kept P1.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

Severity: the filer and the impact skeptic rate P1: every chooser sees the wording, choosing gates the payoff chart, the card path shows no disclaimer, '$0 a month on IBR' sits atop every tab, and borrowers in grace, forbearance or a SAVE notice window have no bill to correct them. The reproduce and standards skeptics rate P2: no misreading was measured, no figure is wrong, and both AI passes rated it P2. All three say a short comprehension test with borrowers would settle it (the impact skeptic suggests five people). Fix: every skeptic replaced the filed fix. Its link reused the page.tsx:401-405 classes, which measure 1.00:1 in the navy hero; its note hung under the pill, which only renders when the chosen plan is our pick, so the card path got nothing; it left the summary line and Payoff header as they are; and its hero-only test passes with those gaps (the as-written build scored 0 of 7 on the stricter test). Evidence: the filer's 'the only hint' overstates, since other hints exist, though none names a step.

Fix choice: labels, link target and placement follow the standards skeptic's scratch build (tsc 0, next build 0, verify.mjs 180 of 180, 12 of 12 on its widened test). Two changes from it, both from the impact skeptic's scratch build (7 of 7 on its test): the sentence says 'To actually get on a plan' instead of 'To switch for real', because 'switch' presumes a current plan while 34 CFR 685.210(a)(1)(i) also covers a first choice; and the summary uses 'if you are on Standard', because the standards build's 'estimated $311 a month on Standard' still states 'on Standard' as fact. The merged set has not been built as one patch. The pill and badge use '✓ In my numbers' because '✓ Using this for my numbers' wrapped to two lines at 320 in the standards build (the reproduce skeptic saw it fit); a card button reading 'Use Tiered Standard for my numbers' wraps to two lines at 375 and stays 48px tall. Link target: the Repayment Calculator, chosen by two of three skeptics because it holds the Apply step for every plan type; https://studentaid.gov/idr/ (the reproduce skeptic's pick) covers income-driven plans only and requires a login. The 'Loan Simulator' name at PlansPanel.tsx:487 and plans.ts:279 is now stale for the federal tool. Coordinate with any kept finding that standardizes labels on 'my plan' (ux-label-inconsistency) or adds aria-label 'Use X as my plan, our recommendation' (agentic-ambiguous-control-names): either would bring the phrase back, and an aria-label that does not contain the new visible label would fail WCAG 2.5.3.
