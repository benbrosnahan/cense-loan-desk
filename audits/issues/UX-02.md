# UX-02: Records says "Nothing changed" when nobody checked

**P2** · effort S · UX and UI

Records tells borrowers to save again after their servicer updates, but the app cannot see the servicer, so a save without retyped balances writes a dated 'Nothing changed' card and a 'No change' spreadsheet row into the record they keep as evidence.

## Measured

Empty state (0 snapshots): 'Save one now to start the record. After your servicer next updates your balances, save another, and this page will show what changed.' The app cannot see a servicer: 0 off-origin requests and no fetch or XHR in src. Following that sentence (8 loans, $28,101, a snapshot from Aug 1, then Save again with nothing retyped): toast 'Snapshot saved', no duplicate guard, newest card 'Nothing changed since Aug 1, 2026 at 2:29 PM.' In the downloaded workbook, read back from disk: 'What changed' F5 = 'No change', 'Snapshots' 'Change vs previous' = 0, and no cell says the figures were typed in. After the first save the empty-state sentence is gone, and a search for type, enter, edit or update matched 0 sentences on the tab at 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440; the only nearby line is 'A snapshot stores every loan exactly as it stands right now, on this device.' (HistoryPanel.tsx:79). 'Nothing changed' and 'No change' also appear after a stored servicer change (Nelnet to MOHELA) or a principal, disbursement date, minimum payment or loan type change, because diffSnapshots compares only balance and rate (storage.ts:91, :101). Snapshot cards hold 0 controls, so a misleading card stays until 'Clear saved records' removes every snapshot. Updating balances today: 4 taps per loan (open row, Edit, focus balance, Save changes), 35 taps and 23 scrolls from Records to a new snapshot at 375x812 with 8 loans (a floor: the on-screen keyboard was not emulated); the route works and shows an 8-row diff.

## Required

Records says, before and after the first save, that a snapshot saves the numbers typed into this app and that the app cannot see the servicer, so new balances must be typed in first. A save with no difference says only that balances and rates match, on screen and in the spreadsheet, and the spreadsheet says its figures were typed by hand. (The filer also asked for a balance update that starts from Records; the skeptics treat that as a separate improvement, see notes.)

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 in a fresh browser and click 'Look around with example loans' (8 loans, $28,101).
2. Open Records and read the empty state: 'After your servicer next updates your balances, save another, and this page will show what changed.'
3. Click 'Save today's snapshot'. Without changing anything, click it again. The toast says 'Snapshot saved' and the newest card says 'Nothing changed since <time of the first save>.' No text on the tab now says balances must be typed in.
4. Click 'Download as a spreadsheet' and open it: on 'What changed', row 5 column F reads 'No change'; on 'Snapshots', 'Change vs previous' is 0.
5. Optional: on Loans, open the first loan, tap Edit, change Servicer from Nelnet to MOHELA and tap 'Save changes'; back on Records, save again. The card still says 'Nothing changed'.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-02"`.

## Fix

Copy only, no new control (built by the impact skeptic in a scratch build: tsc 0, verify.mjs 180 of 180, axe 0 violations).

src/components/history/HistoryPanel.tsx:79-80, the line that stays after the first save:
This app cannot see your servicer. A snapshot saves the balances you have typed here, on this device, so update them first when your statement changes. The spreadsheet is the copy you keep outside the browser.

HistoryPanel.tsx:93-94:
Save one now to start the record. When your servicer&apos;s numbers change, type the new balances in and save another. This page will then show what changed.

HistoryPanel.tsx:127 (the diff compares only balance and rate, so say exactly that; the imperative goes on the newest card only):
```tsx
Balances and rates here match{" "}
{niceDateTime(older.takenAt)}.
{i === 0 &&
  " If your statement shows something different, update your balances and save again."}
```
src/lib/export.ts:
```ts
const PROVENANCE =
  "Every figure here is what was typed into this app on that date. This app cannot see your servicer, so an unchanged number does not mean your servicer's figure stayed the same.";
```
Add [PROVENANCE] as its own row on each sheet: after the description row at :141-143, after the title at :233, and after :260. At :169 replace "No change" with "Same balances and rates" (23 characters; column F is 24 wide at :224, and a one-cell note row overflows across the empty cells beside it).

src/app/page.tsx:820: body="A dated copy of the balances you typed, in case one moves when it should not."

## Severity

It reaches every borrower who saves again without first retyping balances, which is what the empty-state sentence tells them to do, and the card and export then read as if the servicer's figures held. No dollar figure is wrong, the stored numbers are what was typed and the update route works, so it is a misleading statement with no wrong number; the nearby 'on this device' line never says balances must be retyped, so no level is dropped.

## Verification

Confirmed by 3 of 3 skeptics (reproduce, standards, impact); standards and impact corrected severity to P2, reproduce kept P1.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

Severity: the filer and the reproduce skeptic keep P1: following the page's only instruction gives a dated false negative that persists, exports with no qualifier and cannot be removed singly, in the feature built for evidence. The standards and impact skeptics rate P2: the route completes with a correct diff, no figure is wrong, every number was typed by the borrower, and 'Stays on this device' and the footer are on screen; evidence that borrowers expect the app to sync would move it back to P1. Fix: all three corrected the filed copy. 'Same numbers as {date}' is false when principal, servicer, date, payment or type changed; the long xlsx sentence clipped to 'Same numbers as Aug 1, 2026' in a 24-wide column in macOS Quick Look (Excel and Sheets not tested); 'update your balances first' showed on 4 of 5 cards, including old ones; and copy placed only in the empty state disappears after the first save. Evidence: the filer's '4 taps per loan, 32 for eight' were literals in ux-edit.mjs:28, not measurements; measured, 4 per loan is right and the whole journey is 35 taps.

Fix choice: the impact skeptic's copy, which the other two converge on (they used 'Same balances and rates as {date}' on every card; impact keeps the imperative on the newest card because 'first' cannot apply to old ones). The filed 'Update my balances' screen is a separate, optional improvement, effort M: one input per loan and one 'Save and take snapshot' cut 35 taps and 23 scrolls to 10 taps (1 open, 8 field focuses, 1 save) in two scratch builds. If built: do not call saveSnapshot() after setLoans(next), because saveSnapshot closes over the old loans (page.tsx:274-275) and records the old balances (measured: snapshot $28,101 while the hero showed $28,966, and the card reported no difference, recreating this bug); use setSnapshots((prev) => [...prev, makeSnapshot(next)]) and offer Undo through say(message, { label, run }), since a snapshot cannot be deleted singly. Start inputs blank with the saved value shown through <Money>, so Hide amounts blurs it (pre-filled inputs left 8 balances readable); keep a blank field's old number and refuse a typed 0, as LoanForm.tsx:176 does (a cleared pre-filled field stored 0); and add sm:flex-wrap to the button row at HistoryPanel.tsx:66 (three buttons were 74px tall at 640 to 1024 without it). A button that only calls goTo("loans") gives Records a pointer at effort S but keeps the taps. Not tested: how Excel and Google Sheets render the note rows.
