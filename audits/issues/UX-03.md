# UX-03: Next-step cards land mid-section

**P2** · effort S · UX and UI

The Next cards scroll the sticky tab bar into view instead of the new section, so tapping 'See your debt-free date' leaves a phone user 1,340 to 2,690px below the date, looking at the next card instead.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | A borrower following the page's one-next-step sequence taps 'See your debt-free date', lands past both the date and the extra-payment control with the next card in view, and can skip the section the redesign was built around. |
| Standard | No WCAG criterion fails. The rule is the code's own: page.tsx:209 'Start the new section at its top, below the sticky bar', in a page README v3 describes as a sequence where every section ends with a card pointing at the next. The browsers behave as specified (CSS Positioned Layout 3, section 3.4.1, scrolls a stuck box at its offset position), so scrollIntoView on the stuck bar can only move the page by the 80px scroll-padding, and no browser update will fix it. |
| Where | Any width, once the reader has scrolled far enough that the section bar is stuck and then taps a Next card or a section tab. Plans to Payoff ('See your debt-free date') failed in every case measured; Payoff to Records ('Save a dated record') failed in every case, by 52px at 1280 (Save button still visible) and up to 604px on phones; Loans to Plans fails only with 8 loans on phones. Same in Chrome 127, 146, 150 and 156 and in macOS WebKit, on the local build and the live site. From the top of the page the jump works. |
| Code | [`src/app/page.tsx:205-216`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L205-L216), [`src/app/page.tsx:511-512`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L511-L512), [`src/app/page.tsx:545`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L545), [`src/app/globals.css:7`](../../src/app/globals.css#L7) |
| Test | `ux-journeys.spec.mjs`: "UX-03 a Next card or section tab lands with the new section heading below the sticky bar" |
| Fix proof | Not attempted: fix proofs were run for P0 and P1 only. |

## Measured

Section heading hidden above the bar (bar bottom 74px) after the jump. First-run state ($0 extra, no snapshots, plan picked by a real click), Plans to Payoff, where the heading is the 'Debt free in ...' date: 1,340 / 1,518 / 1,778 / 2,394px at 375x812 and 885 / 986 / 1,186 / 1,587px at 1280x800 for 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 loans; 1,617 to 2,690px at 360x640; 1,471 to 2,447px (2.5 to 4.1 screens) at 390x664. In all 12 impact runs the date and the extra-payment control were off screen and the 'Save a dated record' card was fully on screen. Sweep of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8 loans at 375x812, 360x640 and 1280x800: 'See your debt-free date' failed 15 of 15, 'Save a dated record' 15 of 15 (52 to 604px), 'See which repayment plans' 2 of 15 (47 and 217px, both 8 loans). Fixture state (8 loans, $100 extra, 2 snapshots): Payoff heading hidden by 2,658px at 375 and 1,769px at 1280 (5 of 5 repeats with touch, wheel and keyboard), Records by 549 and 189px; macOS WebKit 2,788 and 1,754px. Live site at 375x812 with the 8 example loans: 2,394px. Tapping the lit Payoff tab moves the page 80px per tap; 12 taps did not reach the heading at 390x664 or 1280. Cause: sectionRef is on the sticky bar (page.tsx:511), which once stuck already sits at the viewport top, so scrollIntoView moves the page only by the 5rem scroll-padding (globals.css:7), for example 824 to 744; a jump to a shorter section clamps first. From the top of the page: bar top 80, heading 191 at 375, which is correct.

## Required

After any Next card or section tab press, the new section's first heading sits below the sticky bar (heading top >= bar bottom, 74px) at every width and loan count, as the comment at page.tsx:209 intends.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 in a fresh browser at 375x812 (phone emulation) and click 'Look around with example loans'.
2. Open Plans and click 'Use Standard as my plan' in the navy box. Scroll down to the 'Next: See your debt-free date' card and tap it.
3. The screen shows payoff order rows and the 'One thing that undoes all of this' warning, with the 'Save a dated record' card on screen. The 'Debt free in ...' heading and the extra-payment control are about 2,394px above. Tap the lit Payoff tab: the page moves up only 80px per tap.
4. Scroll down and tap 'Save a dated record': Records opens with 'Save today's snapshot' hidden under the bar.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-03"`.

## Fix

Scroll the non-sticky wrapper the sections render into, not the sticky bar. In src/app/page.tsx, remove ref={sectionRef} from the bar at line 511, and at line 545 change
```tsx
<div className="mt-4">
```
to
```tsx
<div ref={sectionRef} className="mt-4">
```
goTo and globals.css stay as they are: scroll-padding-top: 5rem then puts the section top at 80px, just below the 74px bar. Built in scratch (tsc 0, next build 0): the bar stays stuck at 0-74 and the heading lands at 101px at 375 and 109px at 1280, in Chrome and WebKit; the standards sweep passes 45 of 45 and the impact test 14 of 14.

## Severity

Every borrower who taps 'See your debt-free date' after choosing a plan lands below the date and the extra-payment control in every case measured (1 to 8 loans, 360 to 1280 wide, Chrome and WebKit), with the 'Save a dated record' card on screen inviting them to skip ahead. No figure is wrong, nothing is lost and scrolling up recovers, so the journey does not dead-end: a real but bounded defect under this rubric.

## Verification

Confirmed by 3 of 3 skeptics (standards, impact, reproduce) in Chromium and macOS WebKit; all three kept P1 under the audit brief, regraded P2 under this report's rubric.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

Severity: the filer and all three skeptics rated P1 as a broken key journey under the audit brief, resting on the Plans to Payoff step. This report's rubric reserves P1 for a journey that dead-ends; here every jump completes, no figure is wrong and scrolling up recovers, so it is graded P2. Fix: the filer proposed a zero-height, non-sticky sentinel with scrollMarginTop: '-5rem'. It works (bar 28-102, heading 139 at 375 and 147 at 1280, 45 of 45) but adds a node and a negative margin and leaves the bar unstuck with a strip above it. The impact skeptic replaced it with the wrapper ref; the standards and reproduce skeptics kept the sentinel and offered the wrapper as the simpler option. Evidence: the title's 2,658px is the distance to the H2 in the $100-extra fixture, which reads '$100 extra a month gets you out 3 years 1 month sooner'; the date text there is 2,554px above. The filer's 328 and 281px Loans to Plans figures come from Puppeteer centring the card; tapped as soon as it is visible the miss is 47 to 75px at 375 and 0 to 6px at 1280, and none with 1 to 4 loans. The filer's 'every borrower lands deep' holds for Plans to Payoff only.

Fix choice: the wrapper ref, because it is one moved attribute, keeps the bar stuck at the top, lands 38px higher than the sentinel and needs no negative margin; every skeptic measured it passing. With either fix a long jump's smooth scroll takes about 670 to 1,000ms instead of about 160ms (1,001ms at 320); if that feels slow, use behavior: "auto" in goTo. The standards skeptic also checked that 'Add my own', 'Choose a plan', a same-tab re-press, a one-loan Records page and 200% root font still land with the heading or form below the bar, and that print height is unchanged. One sentinel run under heavy machine load ended 236px low and did not recur in 48 repeats; it is not tied to either fix. Not tested: a real iPhone (macOS WebKit only) and Firefox.
