# UX-05: Plans cannot be compared on one screen

**P2** · effort M · UX and UI

Each open plan's monthly payment, total and term sit in a separate tall card, so a phone never shows more than two plans' figures at once and a borrower cannot see Standard beside RAP without scrolling back and forth.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | A borrower weighing a lower payment against a higher total has to scroll between cards and carry exact figures from one to the next, and on a phone can never see Standard ($311 a month, $37,300 in total) beside RAP ($217 a month, $44,953 in total). |

## Measured

Example balances ($28,101), $52,000 income, private sector, 'Pay the least overall'. Four open plans: Standard $311 a month and $37,300 in total over 10 yr; Graduated $139 to start and $40,646 over 10 yr; IBR $234 and $42,692 over 15.3 yr; RAP $217 and $44,953 over 17.3 yr.
- 375x812: plan titles 376, 457 and 415px apart, 1,248px first to last. The four sets of figures span 1,326px (1,342px counting the bars) against 738px of usable height under the 74px section bar. A scroll sweep finds at most 2 complete sets on screen at any position, and Standard and RAP are never on screen together, titles or figures. Same at 390x844, 430x932 and 320x568.
- 1440x900: titles 282, 340 and 299px apart, 921px first to last; figures 945px (961px with the bars) against 826px usable, so at most 3 sets. Same at 1366x768 and 1024x768; 768x1024 misses by 11px. All four fit only in a window at least 1,019px tall, as at 1920x1080 and 2560x1440.
- Other cases: nonprofit answers stretch the phone span to 1,434px and five open plans to 1,663px; with no income, IBR and RAP show no figures at all. A post-cutoff portfolio with two plans fits (470 of 738px).
- Mitigations already there: the bars share one scale (Standard 100/83, Graduated 45/90, IBR 75/95, RAP 70/100), and each non-top card states its total difference from the pick ('Not picked because it costs $7,654 more overall...' on RAP). No line compares two non-top plans.
- An injected 4-column table in the app's tnum text-sm classes, header plus 4 rows, is 160px tall at 375 with no overflow, both bare (343px) and inside card padding (301px).

## Required

Monthly payment, total and term for every open plan readable together in one phone viewport, as the page's own header comment promises ('every plan side by side').

## Reproduce

1. In a fresh browser window at phone size (375x812), open the app and click 'Look around with example loans'.
2. Tap Plans. Choose 'Pay the least overall' and 'Private sector', and type 52000 in 'Yearly income, before tax'.
3. Scroll through 'All 4 plans open to you'. At no point are Standard's and RAP's figures on screen together, and never more than two plans' 'Per month' and 'In total' figures at once.
These steps were re-checked through the example loans on the local build: the same four plans and figures, with titles 376, 457 and 415px apart.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-05"`.

## Fix

Add a compact comparison table between the intro sentence and the cards, reading the same estimates the cards use, and give each card an anchor the table can link to.

```tsx
// src/components/plans/PlansPanel.tsx, after the intro sentence (line 401), before the card list (line 402):
{open.length > 1 && (
  <table className="mt-3 w-full text-left text-sm">
    <caption className="sr-only">Plans open to you, side by side</caption>
    <thead className="text-xs uppercase tracking-[0.08em] text-slate-700">
      <tr>
        <th scope="col" className="px-2 py-2 font-semibold">Plan</th>
        <th scope="col" className="px-2 py-2 text-right font-semibold">Per month</th>
        <th scope="col" className="px-2 py-2 text-right font-semibold">In total</th>
        <th scope="col" className="px-2 py-2 text-right font-semibold">Over</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      {open.map(({ estimate: e }) => (
        <tr key={e.id} className="border-t border-line">
          <th scope="row" className="px-2 font-semibold">
            <a href={`#card-${e.id}`} className="inline-flex min-h-[40px] items-center text-navy underline underline-offset-2">
              {PLAN_META[e.id].name}
            </a>
          </th>
          {e.monthlyPayment === null ? (
            <td colSpan={3} className="px-2 text-right text-slate-700">Add your income</td>
          ) : (
            <>
              <td className="px-2 text-right">
                <Money value={e.monthlyPayment} />
                {e.id === "graduated" && <span className="block text-xs text-slate-600">to start</span>}
              </td>
              <td className="px-2 text-right"><Money value={e.totalPaid} /></td>
              <td className="tnum px-2 text-right">{years(e.years)}</td>
            </>
          )}
        </tr>
      ))}
    </tbody>
  </table>
)}

// PlanRow's Card (line 113): an anchor for the table link. Not plan-${id}: that id belongs to the detail panel (line 110).
<Card id={`card-${e.id}`} className={cn("overflow-hidden", selected && "border-navy ring-1 ring-navy")}>
```

<Money> keeps every amount under Hide amounts. 'to start' sits on its own line because inline it overflows at 320px. The existing scroll-padding-top: 5rem (globals.css:7) lands each jump below the section bar. The cards, their bars and their 'Not picked because' lines stay as they are.

## Severity

Every borrower with three or more open plans, the usual pre-cutoff case of four or five, meets this on every visit to Plans: at most two plans' figures share a phone screen and three a laptop screen, while post-cutoff borrowers with two plans are not affected. No figure is wrong and the shared-scale bars and 'Not picked because' lines carry part of the comparison, so it is a meaningful missed opportunity on a core function (README.md:16), not a defect in the numbers.
