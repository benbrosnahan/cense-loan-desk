# UX-13: Late-fee notice reads as reassurance

**P2** · effort S · UX and UI

The Loans tab's late-fee notice leads in bold with 'Missing a payment does not cost a late fee', which a skimming borrower can take as permission to skip one, and shows the same text to borrowers already in default, who are never told about collection charges, refund and wage seizure, or rehabilitation.

A borrower already in collections who named the Default Resolution Group sees a pre-default timeline, and nothing says what default is costing them now (collection charges, the whole balance due, tax refund and wage seizure) or how rehabilitation ends it. |

## Measured

Every borrower: the notice's bold title is exactly 'Missing a payment does not cost a late fee' (14px, weight 700, the only bold text in the box), in the info tone ('i' icon, pale green card). The body is 14px regular with no bold, and its first sentence repeats the reassurance. The box is always open: 215px tall at 375 and 126px at 768 and up. The consequences start on body line 3 at 320, line 2 at 375 and line 1 at 768 and up. From the title's top to 'delinquent' is 114px at 320 and 375 and 49px at 768 and up. To '90 days' it is 183px at 320, 137px at 375 and 49px at 768 and up; at 375, '90 days' is on body line 5 of 6. The notice follows the next-step card at every width, including with one loan. The merged AI review rated it P2 on loans-mobile (ai[0]) and loans-desktop (ai[1]), from one sample each.
Print: the notice prints, because no ancestor has no-print. With the 8-loan fixture, the title is left at the foot of page 1 with the body on page 2 at A4 margins of 0.25 to 0.4in and at Letter margins of 0 to 0.1in. A4 at 0.2in splits the body itself. Letter at 0.2 to 1in, which includes the print dialog's usual default of about 0.4in, keeps the whole notice on page 2. With break-inside: avoid injected, the title and '270 days' stayed on one page at all 14 paper and margin combinations tried.
Borrowers in default: with every loan (or 1 of 8) at 'Default Resolution Group', Loans shows the identical info notice (343x215 at 375, 720x126 at 768 and 1440) and no other notice. With every disclosure and loan row opened, the text of all four tabs at all three widths matches a Nelnet control once the servicer name is masked. No tab's text matches collection, accelerat, entire balance, immediately due, offset, refund, treasury, garnish, wages or rehabilit, and the shipped page chunk contains none of garnish, rehabilit, treasury, collection charge, accelerat, tax refund or in default. 'Default' appears only in the servicer name, in 'the loan defaults at 270 days', and twice on Plans as 'the safe default'. No code branches on servicer, and the notice has not changed since the initial commit.

## Required

The bold line is what a skimming reader takes in, so it should carry the consequence as well as the absent fee. It must not claim more than the rule allows: 34 CFR 685.202(d) still lets the Department require a late charge, so the body's 'does not charge' describes current practice and stays as written. The app can identify a borrower as in default when they choose the Default Resolution Group, which FSA says services defaulted loans over 360 days delinquent. That borrower should be told what default costs now and how to end it, not given a pre-default timeline. The costs: the whole balance and interest are due at once (685.211(d)(1)), collection charges are added (685.211(d)(2), 685.202(e)(2)), and the tax refund can be offset and wages garnished (685.211(d)(3)(i)). The way out: rehabilitation, 9 voluntary payments each within 20 days of the due date over 10 consecutive months, ends the default and removes its record (685.211(f)(1), (f)(8)), unless a court judgment exists ((f)(9)). In print, a notice's title should stay on the same page as its body.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 in a fresh browser at phone width (375px) and click 'Look around with example loans'. On the Loans tab, scroll below the 'See which repayment plans you can use' card. The only bold line in the notice reads 'Missing a payment does not cost a late fee', and '90 days' is on the fifth of six regular-weight body lines.
2. Click 'Print one page' and choose A4 with margins of about 0.4in, or Letter with no margins. The title prints at the foot of page 1 and the body on page 2.
3. Default case: click 'Edit' on each loan, set 'Who do you pay?' to 'Default Resolution Group' and click 'Save changes'. Alternatively, in a fresh browser, click 'Add my first loan', tap '2023–24', type 6200 in 'What you owe on it today', choose 'Default Resolution Group' under 'Who do you pay?' and click 'Save loan'. The Loans tab shows the same info notice, and no tab mentions collection charges, garnishment, refund offset or rehabilitation. At 375 the servicer name shows only inside an expanded loan row.
4. Probes.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-13"`.

## Fix

One conditional at the notice covers both cases. Use the names from SCOPE-05's fix (DEFAULT_SERVICER, anyInDefault): whichever issue lands first adds them, and the other reuses them.

1. src/types/index.ts:185: add `export const DEFAULT_SERVICER = "Default Resolution Group";` above SERVICERS and use it in place of the literal.

2. src/lib/calc.ts, after anyParentPlus (:56-58), adding DEFAULT_SERVICER to the "@/types" import:
```ts
/** The Default Resolution Group services only loans already in default. */
export function anyInDefault(loans: Loan[]): boolean {
  return loans.some((l) => l.servicer === DEFAULT_SERVICER);
}
```

3. src/data/plans.ts, after NO_LATE_FEES_NOTE (:275-276), which stays as it is:
```ts
export const IN_DEFAULT_NOTE =
  "Default makes the whole balance and its interest due at once and adds collection charges, and the Department can take your federal tax refund and garnish your wages (34 CFR 685.211(d), 685.202(e)(2)). Rehabilitation ends the default: 9 voluntary monthly payments, each within 20 days of its due date, over 10 consecutive months. Garnished wages and seized refunds do not count toward the 9. It clears the default from your credit history but not the late payments reported before it, it is closed once a court judgment is obtained, and before July 1, 2027 it can be used once per loan (685.211(f)). Consolidating is another way out: see studentaid.gov/manage-loans/default/get-out.";
```

4. src/app/page.tsx: add anyInDefault to the "@/lib/calc" import (:32-39) and IN_DEFAULT_NOTE to the "@/data/plans" import (:26-31). After :185, add `const inDefault = useMemo(() => anyInDefault(loans), [loans]);`. Replace :621-623 with:
```tsx
{inDefault ? (
  <Notice tone="warn" title="Loans with the Default Resolution Group are in default">
    {IN_DEFAULT_NOTE}
  </Notice>
) : (
  <Notice tone="info" title="No late fee, but a missed payment can reach your credit report at 90 days">
    {NO_LATE_FEES_NOTE}
  </Notice>
)}
```

5. src/components/shared/ui.tsx:152: change the root class to `"rounded-2xl border p-4 break-inside-avoid",` so a notice's title prints on the same page as its body. The installed Tailwind 3.4.19 includes break-inside-avoid.

These are local copy and markup changes only: nothing leaves the device and no dependency is added.

## Severity

Borrowers who choose 'Default Resolution Group' as their servicer are a recognizable group, and the form's own servicer list offers that choice. They get a notice that is true word for word but misleading for them: it describes default as a future step and leaves out the costs that apply now, though no wrong number is shown. For every other borrower the bold title is accurate and the consequences sit in the same always-open box, so that part on its own would be P3.

## Verification

Title wording: confirmed by 1 of 2 skeptics (reproduce; standards refuted) and then by the tie-breaker. Default case: confirmed by 2 of 2 skeptics (reproduce, standards). Both members reproduced on the local build and on the live site.
