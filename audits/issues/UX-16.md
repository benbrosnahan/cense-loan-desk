# UX-16: Form messages point the wrong way

**P3** · effort S · UX and UI

The loan form's error messages point to a date field 'below' that sits above them, give a rate range ('between 0 and 25') the check does not follow, and replace each field's hint, including the one-tap 'Use 6.53%' fix, so a borrower correcting the form is sent the wrong way.

| Standard | Nielsen heuristic 9 (help users recognize, diagnose and recover from errors). No WCAG failure is claimed: each error is identified in text and says what to enter. |

## Measured

After an empty Save at 375px (document y): the school-year buttons sit at 583-679, the date input at 717-765 and the message '! Pick the school year, or enter the date below.' at 771-811, with no date field below it (at 1440: 547-591, 629-677 and 683-703). Rate 0 and 0.00 are rejected with 'Enter the interest rate, a number between 0 and 25.'; 25.01 and 26 are rejected; 0.01, 0.5 and 25 save (the check at LoanForm.tsx:178 is rate > 0 && rate <= 25). Balance 0 and 0.00 are rejected. The empty balance and rate fields show the placeholders '0' and '0.00', the values those errors reject. The date, balance and rate hints show before Save and are gone after it, because ui.tsx:266-284 renders the error in place of the hint; after tapping 2024–25 and clearing the rate, leaving the field swaps the line 'The published undergraduate rate for 2024–25 was 6.53%. Use 6.53%' for the error, so the one-tap fix disappears. Typed in desktop Chromium, 09010022 saves as 0022-09-01 (row '22–23 subsidized', 'paid out Sep 1, 22') and 09011950 saves, with no message on either. A Minimum monthly payment of $123 is stored and shown on no tab; estimatePlans and compareStrategies give identical results at 0 and 500, and a grep finds the value read only at export.ts:53.

## Required

Each message says where the control is and states the rule the check applies; a field's hint, including the 'Use 6.53%' shortcut, stays visible beside its error; empty fields do not show a value the form rejects; a year no loan can have is blocked (MONEY-12) and an unlikely one is questioned; a field the borrower is asked to fill says what the number is used for.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 at 375px wide in a fresh browser profile, click 'Add my first loan', then click 'Save loan' with nothing filled in. Under the date field it reads '! Pick the school year, or enter the date below.', while the year buttons and the date field are both above it. The hints under the date, balance and rate fields are gone, and the empty balance and rate fields read '0' and '0.00'.
2. Type 0 in 'Interest rate' and tap elsewhere: '! Enter the interest rate, a number between 0 and 25.' shows and Save stays blocked.
3. Tap 2024–25 (the rate fills in 6.53), clear the rate, and note the line 'The published undergraduate rate for 2024–25 was 6.53%. Use 6.53%'. Tap elsewhere: the error replaces that line and its button.
4. In Chromium, type 09011950 (or 09010022) in 'Date it was paid out', fill in a balance and a rate, and save: no message appears and the loan is saved.
5. Add a loan, open 'Add more detail', type 123 under 'Minimum monthly payment' and save. 123 appears on no tab; it shows up only in the 'Minimum monthly payment' column of the spreadsheet from the Loans tab.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-16"`.

## Fix

Six small changes in two files. Ship with MONEY-12, which rewrites the same lines (LoanForm.tsx:172-181).

1. Date message, LoanForm.tsx:175: "Pick the school year or enter the date above." Use the same words in MONEY-12's empty-date branch, whose snippet still says "below".

2. Rate message, LoanForm.tsx:180, matching the check at :178 (one wording for MONEY-22 and RES-14 too): "Enter the interest rate, above 0 and up to 25."

3. Delete placeholder="0" at :340 and placeholder="0.00" at :386, so the empty balance and rate fields stop showing values the form rejects.

4. Field, ui.tsx:266-284: show the error above the hint instead of in its place, inside one element that keeps the `${htmlFor}-msg` id the inputs reference with aria-describedby (two paragraphs would duplicate the id):
```tsx
<div id={htmlFor ? `${htmlFor}-msg` : undefined}>
  {error && (
    <p
      role="alert"
      className="mt-1.5 flex items-start gap-1.5 text-sm font-semibold text-red-800"
    >
      <span aria-hidden>!</span>
      {error}
    </p>
  )}
  {hint && <p className="mt-1.5 text-sm leading-snug text-slate-600">{hint}</p>}
</div>
```
Change the comment at ui.tsx:247 to "Shown above the hint, and announced, once the field has a problem." This keeps 'Use 6.53%' on screen beside a rate error.

5. Dates: MONEY-12 blocks years no loan can have (before 1965 or more than two years ahead, which covers 0022 and 1950). For a year that passes that rule but is unlikely, ask without blocking, in the date hint at :289-293:
```tsx
const disbYear = Number(f.disbursementDate.slice(0, 4)); // MONEY-12 adds this above errors
// after errors (:181):
const askYear =
  !errors.disbursementDate &&
  (disbYear < 1990 || disbYear > new Date().getFullYear() + 1);
// date Field:
hint={
  <>
    {askYear && `Is ${disbYear} the right year? `}
    {pickedYear
      ? "We used September 1. If studentaid.gov shows a different disbursement date, change it here."
      : "Called the disbursement date on studentaid.gov."}
  </>
}
```

6. Minimum monthly payment, LoanForm.tsx:480: keep the field and say what happens to the number today: hint="Goes into your spreadsheet only. The figures on these pages do not use it." When SCOPE-06 starts reading it as the borrower's bill, replace this hint with what it is used for.

No dependency; nothing leaves the device.

## Severity

Most borrowers who add a loan see at least one of these messages, because validation runs on leaving a field and on Save, but each message sits directly under its own field, focus already moves to the first problem, and no figure or eligibility answer changes. The wrong-year case that does change eligibility is filed separately as MONEY-12 (P1).
