# UX-17: One thing has several names across tabs

**P3** · effort M · UX and UI

The app calls one thing by different names on different tabs ('snapshot' and 'record', 'paid out' and 'disbursed', 'Where we would start' and 'Our pick'), gives two different downloads the same label, and titles four warnings with phrases like 'Watch this one', so borrowers have to work out what each word means.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | A stressed reader moving between tabs has to work out whether 'snapshot' and 'record', 'paid out' and 'disbursed', or 'Where we would start' and 'Our pick' are the same thing, and cannot tell the two 'Download as a spreadsheet' buttons apart without opening the files. |
| Standard | Nielsen heuristic 4 (consistency and standards) and plain-language practice. No WCAG failure is claimed. |
| Where | Loans, Plans, Payoff and Records at every width (counted at 375 and 1440 with every disclosure open). 'Watch this one' shows only for public-service borrowers, and 'Check this before anything else' and 'One date decided this' only once a loan is paid out on or after July 1, 2026. |

## Measured

Counted in rendered text with every disclosure open, on the eight-loan fixture with a nonprofit employer. Plans at 375: 'Where we would start' 1, 'your plan' 2 (the card badge '✓ Your plan' and the dark panel's '✓ This is your plan'), 'my plan' 4 (the buttons), 'paid out' 1, 'disbursed' 2, 'portfolio' 1, 'Watch this one' 1. 'Close to the top pick on every measure.' renders on the same screen as the 'Our pick' badge (income 90,000, nonprofit, goal forgiveness) and fired 13 times across 56 income, goal and employer combinations. Records at 375: 'record' 4, 'snapshot' 2. Storage names at one width: 'On your device' (the header pill below 640px, which reads 'Stays on this device' at 1440), 'this browser' and 'on this device'. 'portfolio' appears on Plans and Payoff, and on Loans after the cutoff. With a ninth loan paid out after July 1, 2026: 'Check this before anything else' on Loans, 'One date decided this' and 'disbursed' 6 times on Plans; 'One thing that undoes all of this' titles the Payoff notice in every state. 'Watch this one' titles the PSLF warning on Graduated, and also on Extended over $30,000 and on Tiered Standard after the cutoff. Both export buttons read 'Download as a spreadsheet', checked with real clicks: Loans saves cense-loan-desk-2026-11-02.xlsx (sheet My loans) and Records saves cense-loan-history-2026-11-02.xlsx (What changed, Snapshots, All loan rows). A case-insensitive grep for 'income[- ]based' in src finds nothing, so IBR is never spelled out, while RAP's summary opens 'The Repayment Assistance Plan.' (plans.ts:189).

## Required

One name for each thing on every tab, titles that state their point, plain words instead of 'portfolio', IBR spelled out once, and download labels that say which file each one saves.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 at 375px wide and click 'Look around with example loans'. Open Repayment plans, choose 'Nonprofit' under 'Who do you work for, or expect to?' and type a yearly income.
2. Plans: the dark panel's label reads 'Where we would start' while the same plan's card carries 'Our pick'. Tap a card's 'Use … as my plan': that card now shows the badge '✓ Your plan' above the button '✓ This is my plan'. The Graduated card shows a warning titled 'Watch this one'. Open 'Two rules worth knowing about multiple loans': 'Every loan you entered was disbursed before July 1, 2026 … would change this for your whole portfolio.' Open IBR's details: its summary never says 'Income-Based Repayment', while RAP's opens 'The Repayment Assistance Plan.'
3. Your loans: the rows say 'paid out'. Under 'Keep a copy', 'Download as a spreadsheet' saves cense-loan-desk-<date>.xlsx with one sheet, My loans.
4. Records: the tab and heading say 'record', the button says 'Save today's snapshot' and the empty state 'No snapshots yet'. Save one; this tab's 'Download as a spreadsheet' saves cense-loan-history-<date>.xlsx (What changed, Snapshots, All loan rows).
5. Payoff ends with a notice titled 'One thing that undoes all of this'.
6. Add a loan dated 09/01/2026: Loans shows 'Check this before anything else' ('That limits your entire portfolio …'), and Plans shows 'One date decided this'.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-17"`.

## Fix

Copy only, about 27 strings in 7 files; no figure changes. One name per thing, matching what the borrower sees most and what other issues already write.

1. Recommendation, 'Our pick': PlansPanel.tsx:346, the panel label 'Where we would start' becomes 'Our pick'; calc.ts:588 'Close to the top pick on every measure.' becomes 'Close to our pick on every measure.'

2. Plan choice: use UX-01's labels ('✓ In my numbers', 'Use {name} for my numbers' at PlansPanel.tsx:124, :208, :365 and :373). They give the choice one name, and UX-01 retires both 'your plan' and 'my plan'.

3. Loan date, 'paid out', keeping the two form hints at LoanForm.tsx:291-292 that tie it to studentaid.gov's 'disbursement date': page.tsx:552 'At least one loan was paid out on or after July 1, 2026.'; LoanForm.tsx:501 'Paid out on or after July 1, 2026.'; PlansPanel.tsx:464 'Every loan you entered was paid out before July 1, 2026'; calc.ts:295 'Closed because one of your loans was paid out on or after July 1, 2026'; the same swap in plans.ts:162, :176, :258 and :270.

4. History, 'record': HistoryPanel.tsx:68 "Save today's record"; :79 'A record stores every loan …'; :82 '… once you have saved a record.'; :90 'No records yet'; the toast at page.tsx:276 'Record saved'.

5. Storage, 'this browser': take RES-06's pill ('In this browser' and 'Stays in this browser' at page.tsx:337-338) and its 'in this browser' at HistoryPanel.tsx:79.

6. Downloads: page.tsx:634 'Download my loans'; HistoryPanel.tsx:75 'Download my records'.

7. Titles that state the point. PlansPanel.tsx:195 is computed, because that notice renders for any plan whose PSLF status is not 'counts':
```tsx
<Notice
  tone="warn"
  className="mt-3.5"
  title={`${meta.name} ${e.pslf === "partial" ? "only partly counts" : "does not count"} for PSLF`}
>
  {e.pslfNote}
</Notice>
```
PlansPanel.tsx:324 'What your loan dates decide'; page.tsx:551 'A loan date limits your plans'; RankingPanel.tsx:497 'Tell your servicer where extra goes'.

8. 'portfolio', rewritten per sentence: page.tsx:554-555 and LoanForm.tsx:502 'limits all your loans to RAP and Tiered Standard'; PlansPanel.tsx:467-468 'would change this for all your loans.'; plans.ts:258 and :270 'across all your loans, including the ones you took out earlier'; RankingPanel.tsx:432 'and with balances this size it is usually smaller than people expect.'

9. plans.ts:147: start IBR's summary with 'Income-Based Repayment. Caps your payment at 10% of discretionary income, …', as RAP's opens 'The Repayment Assistance Plan.'

Leave the spreadsheet headings and sheet names (export.ts:38, :40 and :235) as they are. No dependency; nothing leaves the device.

## Severity

Every borrower who moves between tabs meets several of these names in one visit, but no figure or answer is wrong and each screen still makes sense on its own; the cost is reading effort and doubt, which is polish under this rubric.

## Verification

Confirmed by 1 of 1 skeptic (P3 review lens), who recounted every term in rendered text at 375 and 1440 with every disclosure open, in a normal and a post-cutoff portfolio, downloaded both files with real clicks, and checked every cited line.
