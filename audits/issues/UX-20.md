# UX-20: No sources or "rules checked" date on screen

**P2** · effort S · UX and UI

No screen says when the 2026 loan rules were last checked or links to any regulation, and the first-run headline claims something the next card contradicts, so borrowers cannot verify where the figures come from.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | A borrower choosing a repayment plan cannot tell whether the figures reflect the rules in force since July 1, 2026, or open the rule behind them, and the first claim they can test, on the first screen, is overstated. |
| Standard | No pass/fail standard. Stanford Web Credibility Guidelines 1 (make accuracy verifiable through cited sources) and 8 (show the content was recently reviewed). Google Search Central's helpful-content self-assessment weighs trust most on topics that affect financial stability and asks about clear sourcing, but it does not ask for a visible date. |
| Where | Every state and tab at every width (checked at 375 and 1024px on localhost:3100, and on the live site): the footer on every screen (page.tsx:839-859), the first-run headline and its 'What you will need' card, the add-loan form intro, the closed 'Two rules worth knowing about multiple loans' disclosure on Plans, and the GAO sentence on Records. |
| Code | [`src/app/page.tsx:843`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L843), [`src/app/page.tsx:26`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L26), [`src/app/page.tsx:363`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L363), [`src/app/page.tsx:400`](../../src/app/page.tsx#L400), [`src/components/loans/LoanForm.tsx:249`](../../src/components/loans/LoanForm.tsx#L249), [`src/data/plans.ts:281`](../../src/data/plans.ts#L281), [`src/data/plans.ts:285`](../../src/data/plans.ts#L285), [`src/data/plans.ts:273`](../../src/data/plans.ts#L273) |

## Measured

No last-checked date anywhere: across 28 render states (six fixture states at 375 and 1024px; Parent PLUS, post-cutoff and combined portfolios on all four tabs; two form notices; two example-loan tabs; disclosures open and closed) there are 0 matches for checked, reviewed, updated, verified, revised, 'as of' or 'current as/through', and 0 <time> elements, date meta tags or JSON-LD. No rule source is linked: src has 5 hrefs (page.tsx:317, 402, 849; LoanForm.tsx:242; PlansPanel.tsx:483) that render as 4 values (#main, studentaid.gov, studentaid.gov/loan-simulator/, cense.beehiiv.com), and 0 point to eCFR, the Federal Register or GAO. Citations rarely reach the screen: of 36 distinct 685.x citations in src, verify.mjs and README (25 in src), 3 are ever rendered, all unlinked and conditional: 34 CFR 685.210(a)(3) inside the closed 'Two rules worth knowing about multiple loans' disclosure on Plans, 685.209(d) when a Parent PLUS loan is saved or picked in the form, and 685.210 in the form after a post-cutoff date. A plain portfolio's default view shows none. README.md:22-29 lists 7 sources and 0 of 7 are linked; 685.208, 91 FR 23768, the fsapartners rate announcements and the GAO report number never appear on screen. Records cites the GAO's March 2026 finding with no report number or link. The footer calls studentaid.gov 'the authority on your account' in plain text. The first-run headline says eight loans and 'nowhere that shows them together', while the card below says all three numbers are at studentaid.gov under My Aid, and the form adds 'Each loan has its own row.' plans.ts:69 cites 685.208(e), which does not exist: 685.208 has only (a), (b) and (c), and the $30,000 Extended test is at (b)(4)(i). That is a code comment, and the $30,000 is correct. The rules did change this year: eCFR shows 685.208, .209, .210 and .219 amended on 2026-05-01 and 2026-07-01. The live site matches local: a page chunk of the same size (101,571 bytes) with 4 '34 CFR' strings, 5 hrefs and no rule link or freshness string.

## Required

A visible date when a person last checked the rules, linked sources a borrower can open (section-level links at minimum), and no claim on the first screen that the next card contradicts.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 with no saved data. The headline paragraph says four years usually means eight federal loans 'and nowhere that shows them together'; the card right below says all three numbers are at studentaid.gov under My Aid. Tap 'Add my first loan': the form says 'Each loan has its own row.'
2. Scroll to the footer: it names studentaid.gov as the authority on your account, in plain text, with no date and no source.
3. Tap Cancel, tap 'Look around with example loans', and visit Loans, Plans, Payoff and Records, opening every disclosure. No screen says when the rules were checked. The only regulation citation, 34 CFR 685.210(a)(3), sits inside the closed 'Two rules worth knowing about multiple loans' disclosure on Plans, unlinked. Records cites the Government Accountability Office's March 2026 finding with no report number or link.
4. Optional: tap 'Add a loan' and pick 'Parent PLUS' or the '2026–27' year. The form notice cites 34 CFR 685.209(d) or 685.210, unlinked.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-20"`.

## Fix

Steps 1 and 2 are the standards skeptic's self-contained version, built and measured; steps 3 and 4 are one-line edits.

1. src/data/plans.ts, after PRIVACY_NOTE (line 285):

/** The date a person last read these sections against this code. Never the build date. */
export const RULES_CHECKED = "<the date you last checked>";

export const RULE_SOURCES: { label: string; href: string }[] = [
  { label: "34 CFR 685.208: fixed-payment plans, including Tiered Standard", href: "https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.208" },
  { label: "34 CFR 685.209: IBR and RAP", href: "https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209" },
  { label: "34 CFR 685.210: choosing a plan, and the July 1, 2026 cutoff", href: "https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.210" },
  { label: "34 CFR 685.219: Public Service Loan Forgiveness", href: "https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.219" },
  { label: "Final rule, May 1, 2026 (91 FR 23768)", href: "https://www.federalregister.gov/citation/91-FR-23768" },
  { label: "2026 poverty guidelines (HHS)", href: "https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines" },
  { label: "GAO-26-108534: servicer accuracy", href: "https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108534" },
];

2. src/app/page.tsx: add RULES_CHECKED and RULE_SOURCES to the @/data/plans import (lines 26-31; Disclosure is already imported). After the NOT_ADVICE paragraph (line 845):

<p className="mt-2 text-sm text-slate-600">
  Rules last checked {RULES_CHECKED}.
</p>
<Disclosure summary="Where these rules come from" className="mt-1">
  <ul className="space-y-1 text-sm">
    {RULE_SOURCES.map((s) => (
      <li key={s.href}>
        <a
          href={s.href}
          target="_blank"
          rel="noopener noreferrer"
          className="inline-block min-h-[40px] py-2 font-semibold text-navy underline underline-offset-2 hover:text-navy-hover"
        >
          {s.label}
        </a>
      </li>
    ))}
  </ul>
</Disclosure>

3. src/app/page.tsx:362-363, drop the claim the next card contradicts, so the paragraph reads: "Four years of school usually means eight separate federal loans. Add yours once to see what you owe, which repayment plans you can actually use, and what extra money would change."

4. src/data/plans.ts:69, correct the comment's citation to "Source: 34 CFR 685.208(b)(4)(i)."

Measured on a scratch build of steps 1 and 2 with a placeholder date: the route grew from 37.2 kB to 37.6 kB and First Load JS stayed at 125 kB. In six states at 320, 375 and 1024px the date line shows and the disclosure opens to 7 links (4 eCFR, 1 Federal Register), all underlined, at least 40px tall, opening in a new tab with noopener noreferrer. axe WCAG A/AA on the footer: 0 violations at 375 and 1024px. 0 console errors, and no new page overflow (the widest link ends at 304px at 320px).

## Severity

Every visitor, on every tab and at every width, sees no last-checked date and no link to any rule source on a tool whose governing CFR sections changed in 2026 (latest 2026-07-01), and every first-run visitor reads a headline the next card contradicts. No figure is wrong, so this is a misleading statement and a meaningful missed opportunity for trust, not P1.
