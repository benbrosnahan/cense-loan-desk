# UX-21: Loan rows repeat the type and hide the servicer on phones

**P3** · effort S · UX and UI

On phones each loan row repeats the type already in its name, hides the servicer, and starts the rate at two different positions, so borrowers must open rows to see who holds each loan and compare rates along a ragged edge.

| | |
|---|---|
| Who it hurts | A borrower with loans at two servicers has to open each row on a phone to see who holds it, and compares rates along a ragged edge. |
| Standard | No pass/fail standard. PerfMaster's AI UX pass flagged it on loans-desktop and loans-mobile; the lead downgraded it because each row opens to show the rest. |
| Where | Loans tab, every loan row. Servicer and date are hidden below 640px (measured at 375x812 and 639px); the repeated type and the two rate offsets also appear at 640 and 1440px. |

## Measured

At 375, 8 of 8 fixture rows repeat the type: '2022–23 subsidized' over 'Subsidized · 4.99%'. The real flow does the same, because a loan saved with a blank name is auto-named from its school year and type. The rate's left edge sits at 109.98px or 126.55px depending on whether 'Subsidized' or 'Unsubsidized' comes before it, a 16.57px spread (measured with a Range on the rate text); the same two offsets appear at 640 (117.98/134.55) and 1440 (453.98/470.55). Servicer and paid-out date show on 0 of 8 rows at 639px and 8 of 8 at 640px; an opened row shows 'You pay Nelnet' and 'Paid out Sep 1, 2022'. The shipped row already clips the 'after Jul 1, 2026' tag by up to 24px at 375.

## Required

Rates line up in one column, the row does not repeat what the name already says, the servicer is visible on the phone row without opening it, and the post-cutoff tag stays readable.

## Reproduce

1. Open http://localhost:3100 at 375x812 with no saved data and tap 'Look around with example loans' (or add loans with the Name left blank: a 2022–23 subsidized loan is auto-named '2022–23 subsidized').
2. On the Loans tab, read the rows: 'Freshman subsidized' over 'Subsidized · 4.99%', then 'Freshman unsubsidized' over 'Unsubsidized · 4.99%'. The type repeats, no servicer or date is shown, and the two 4.99% values start at different x positions.
3. Tap a row: 'You pay Nelnet' and the paid-out date appear only inside it.
4. Widen the window to 640px: servicer and date join each row, and the rates still start at two offsets.

Or run its test from `audits/`: `AUDIT_SHOW_KNOWN=1 npx playwright test -g "UX-21"`.

## Fix

Rewrite the meta line at LoanList.tsx:57-62 so the rate comes first, then the post-cutoff tag, then the servicer at every width, the paid-out date from 640px up, and the type last, only when the name does not already contain it as a whole word:

{pct(l.rate)}
{isPostCutoff(l) && " · after Jul 1, 2026"}
{" · "}{l.servicer}
<span className="hidden sm:inline print:inline">
  {" · "}paid out {niceDate(l.disbursementDate)}
</span>
{!new RegExp(`\\b${LOAN_KIND_SHORT[l.kind]}\\b`, "i").test(l.nickname) &&
  ` · ${LOAN_KIND_SHORT[l.kind]}`}

The whole-word test matters: 'subsidized' is inside 'unsubsidized', and a loan whose type is edited to Subsidized keeps the name '2022–23 unsubsidized'. The servicer moves out of the sm:inline span so it shows once at every width.

Measured on a scratch build: verify and next build exit 0. An auto-named row reads '4.99% · Nelnet'; 'Loan 1' reads '4.99% · Nelnet · Subsidized'; a subsidized loan named '2022–23 unsubsidized' keeps 'Subsidized'; '2022–23 parent plus' hides the type. Rates share one left offset at 375, 639, 640 and 1440px, custom-named rows included. The servicer shows once on 8 of 8 rows at 639, 640 and 1440px. The post-cutoff tag is clipped on 0 of 16 rows at 375 and 320px.

## Severity

Every borrower who views the Loans tab (the default tab) on a screen narrower than 640px. Nothing is wrong or lost, because opening a row shows the servicer and date, so this is scanning polish.

## Verification

Confirmed by 1 of 1 skeptic (P3 review lens), who reproduced it at 375, 639, 640 and 1440px and built and measured two orderings of the fix.

<details><summary>Dissent and notes</summary>

The skeptic confirmed the defect and kept P3 but corrected three details of the member's fix; all three are applied above. (1) A plain 'contains' test would hide the only correct label on a loan whose type was edited after auto-naming, because 'subsidized' is inside 'unsubsidized' (a direct_sub loan named '2022–23 unsubsidized' was measured), so the test is whole-word. (2) The member's order, servicer before the post-cutoff tag, clips the tag on long servicer names: 8 of 16 post-cutoff rows at 375px, by up to 100.5px for Default Resolution Group, and all 16 at 320px, against at most 24px in the shipped row. With the tag first it is clipped on 0 of 16 at both widths. (3) The servicer has to leave the sm:inline span, or it shows twice from 640px up.

With the tag first, what truncates on a narrow phone is the servicer or the type, never the eligibility tag. The skeptic's extras run also shows the shipped row clipping that tag by 8px and 24px at 375px and by 63px and 79px at 320px, so the new order fixes that too. A loan whose servicer was left as 'Not sure yet' will read '4.99% · Not sure yet' on a phone, the same words wider screens show today. Single-member issue. No new dependency, and nothing leaves the device.

</details>
